This evening I saw the movie *[Alice in Wonderland](http://www.apple.com/trailers/disney/aliceinwonderland/ "Alice in Wonderland trailer")* in 3D at AMC Cinemas, Pacific Place, Admiralty, Hong Kong. One of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/mustsee) list.

This Tim Burton sequel to the original [Alice in Wonderland](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0000420/ "Alice in Wonderland (1903) details") movie from 1903, began the story with a 19 years old Alice Kingsleigh who just lost her father and is about to be married in an arranged marriage with the son of his father’s ex-partner. Alice ([Mia Wasikowska](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm1985859/ "Mia Wasikowska Profile Details")), got distracted and followed the White Rabbit ([Michael Sheen](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0790688/ "Michael Sheen profile details")) into a rabbit hole once more and began her adventure in Wonderland.

In Wonderland there is a battle between the Red Queen ([Helena Bonham Carter](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000307/ "Helena Bonham Carter profile details")) and the White Queen ([Anne Hathaway](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0004266/ "Anne Hathaway profile details")), in which Red Queen won and took over Wonderland. The White Rabbit was sent by Dormouse ([Barbara Windsor](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0934774/ "Barbara Windsor profile details")), Dodo bird (voice of [Michael Gough](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001284/ "Michael Gough profile details")), and Tweedledee / Tweedledum ([Matt Lucas](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0524240/ "Matt Lucas profile details")) to look for Alice, because The Scroll claims that on the Frabjous Day, Alice will return to slay the Jabberwocky ([Christopher Lee](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000489/ "Christopher Lee profile details")). Along the way Alice runs into her old friend Hatter ([Johnny Depp](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000136/ "Johnny Depp profile details")) and he further explains to Alice what she needs to do.

Carter gave a familiar performance as she did with her character in *[Sweeney Todd](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0408236/ "Sweeney Todd details")*, which Depp starred as Sweeney Todd. The Australian actress Wasikowska played a wonderful Alice, someone innocent, determine, ambitious and full of imaginations.

The special effect was okay and the 3D affects were minimal. I would not suggest going to see the 3D version of the film, as I believe the extra cost is not worth it. Rather I wish I had simply chosen a theater with a large screen and good sound system.
