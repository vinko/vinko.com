This evening I went to see the movie “[The Pink Panther](http://www.apple.com/trailers/sony_pictures/thepinkpanther/index.html)” at UA Cinema, Time Square.

I had always like the Pink Panther movie series with [Peter Sellers](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000634/) back in the 1970’s and 1963:

1. [The Pink Panther](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0057413/)
2. [The Return of the Pink Panther](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0072081/)
3. [The Pink Panther Strikes Again](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0075066/)
4. [Revenge of the Pink Panther](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0078163/)

This new movie had the same spirit and I cannot think of any one better to play the role of “Inspector Jacques Clouseau” than [Steve Martin](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000188/).

For the Pink Panther fanboys this is definitely another must have for your DVD collection. Unfortunately, if I talk about the plot of this movie too much I will ruin it for you. That’s one of the reason why I have I only gave this movie 3 penguins.
