![Smartone-Vodafone logo](http://vinko.files.wordpress.com/2008/03/smartone-vodafone_logo.gif)All of a sudden I received the following cryptic SMS message from Smartone-Vodafone:

> Voicemail system migration has been completed. Your greeting & PIN have been reset. To check messages before system migration, dial 138 & press 5.

The thing is that there was no prior message to say that the Voicemail system is to be migrated. Also what does the last sentence mean? Isn’t this SMS to inform me that the said migration has already been completed?

Seeing that this Voicemail “migration” comes so close to the official iPhone launch by Smartone-Vodafone on the 23rd. Does it imply that I’m correct earlier about [Apple’s Visual Voicemail coming to SMV iPhone customers](http://blog.vinko.com/2010/01/15/tethering-finally-returns-smv-iphone-users-hk/ "Tethering Finally Returns to SMV iPhone Users") very soon?
