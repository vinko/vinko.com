This afternoon I saw the movie *[Knowing](http://www.apple.com/trailers/summit/knowing/ "Knowing trailer")* at AMC Theater, Pacific Place, Admiralty, Hong Kong. Another one of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.php) list.

After watching the poor performance of [Dwayne Johnson](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0425005/ "Dwayne Johnson profile") in [*Race to Witch Mountain*](http://blog.vinko.com/2009/04/05/movie-review-race-to-witch-mountain/ "Race to Witch Mountain review") last week, I was glad to watch a seasoned actor, [Nicolas Cage](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000115/ "Nicolas Cage profile"), delivering a great performance. Although Cage’s performance is only great when compared to Johnson, here Cage was playing his “[Ben Gates](http://www.imdb.com/character/ch0007102/ "Ben Gates profile")” character from [*National Treasure*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0368891/ "National Treasure  details").

The thing that earned this movie an extra penguin in my rating was the story and the twists in the plot. It did not have as complicated of a plot as [*National Treasure*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0368891/ "National Treasure  details"), but contains enough suspense for the audience to want more. Without breaking my rule of no spoilers, there is really not much more I can say about the movie.

I can leave you with the following detail, as with [*I Am Legend*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0480249/) the ending of this movie is a bit unexpected.
