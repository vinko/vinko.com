I just saw the movie “[Along Came Polly](http://www.apple.com/trailers/universal/along_came_polly)” at Broadway Olympian Theatre this evening, starring “Ben Stiller” and “Jennifer Aniston”.

It is a good funny love story. A truely enjoyable evening out. Ben Stiller performs his usual best underdog who ends up winning the woman resulting in a happy ending for all.
