Water occupies 71% of the Earth but we still have millions of people drinking dirty water. Some of these people (approximately 1.8 billion) walk miles to get these dirty water. Are there no solution to this atrocity?

Michael Pritchard has the invention, [LifeSaver](http://www.ted.com/talks/michael_pritchard_invents_a_water_filter), that can be the answer. In 2007 Michael received the “Best Technological Development” award for his invention and publicly showed off his invention in a [TED Talk](http://www.ted.com/talks/michael_pritchard_invents_a_water_filter) in 2009.

Since then Michael’s company [LifeSaver Systems Ltd.](http://www.lifesaversystems.com) has partnered with [NGO](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-governmental_organization)s and militaries around the world to help people receive clean drinking water. Including major disasters like [2004 Asian Tsunami](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Asian_Tsunami), [Hurricane Katrina](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Katrina) and [2015 Nepal earthquake](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_2015_Nepal_earthquake).

Although, there is a lifespan for the filters used within these LifeSaver systems, with appropriate funding I think we can achieve the UN goal of no one live without clean drinking water.
