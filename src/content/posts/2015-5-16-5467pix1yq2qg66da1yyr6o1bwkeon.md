This evening I saw the movie [*Spy*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt3079380/) at UA Cine Times, Times Square, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. One of the movies from my Must See Movies list, and the following is my movie review.

I had not written a movie review for some time, not to say that I had not been watching movies. In my opinion this was the best movie I watched this year; even after big names like *[Avengers: Age of Ultron](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt2395427/),**[Furious Seven (2015)](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt2820852/)* and[*Still Alice*](#). That was saying a lot since I watch a lot of movies, both at the theater and via iTunes.

[Melissa McCarthy](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0565250) was very good. Something I had expect, ever since I watched her performance in *[Identity Thief](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt2024432/)*with the not so great[Jason Bateman](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000867/?ref_=tt_cl_t1). This time around Melissa was partnered with two famous male actors: [Jason Statham](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0005458/) and [Jude Law](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000179/), who both have not had blockbusters movies for a while. This time around Jude played a pretty boy James Bond type who didn’t really have any talents, Jason played a guy with tough exterior, all-talk but soft inside.

Melissa’s natural stand-up comic side came out throughout the movie. I can imagine how enjoyable it would have been for the other actors on set. Making the director’s job even harder to stay on track as [Paul Feig](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0082450/) had envisioned it.

If you’re looking for a fun night out this is definitely a sure bet.
