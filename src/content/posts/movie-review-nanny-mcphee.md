This evening I went to see the movie “ [Nanny McPhee](http://www.apple.com/trailers/universal/nanny_mcphee/)” at UA Cinema, Time Square.

Since I have not read the book I do not know how accurate was the screenplay. Given this I enjoy the movie, particularly with Emma Thompson’s performance. The whole thing reminds me of [Harry Potter](http://www.imdb.com/find?s=tt&q=harry+potter) and [Edward Scissorhands](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0099487/).

It does have all the elements to entertain both kids and kids at heart.
