Today I went to visit the “[Refabricating City 2008](http://www.hkszbiennale.asia)” exhibition at the Hollywood Police Station, 10 Hollywood Road, Central, Hong Kong.

It showed off some of the urban development concepts from both the Hong Kong and Shenzhen city urban development.

You can check out some of the photos from my [Flickr album](http://flickr.com/photos/vinkotsui/sets/72157603804815261/ "Refabricating City 2008 Photo Album").
