![Beijing Olympic 2008 logo](/wp-content/uploads/2008/07/olympic_logo_beijing_2008.gif)It is surprising how difficult to find the latest official updates of medal counts from the Beijing 2008 Olympic.

After a Google search and ignoring all the scammers who try to make AdSense income, I finally found a list at [Yahoo Sports](http://sports.yahoo.com/olympics/beijing/medals "Beijing 2008 Olympic Medal List"), but the one that is most up-to-date is the [2008 Olympic Medal Count on Wikipedia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Summer_Olympics_medal_count "2008 Olympic Medal Count"). This further proves that people-power on the Internet is much faster and better, and [Crowdsourcing](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crowdsourcing "Definition of Crowdsourcing") is a practical and achievable future for project development.

As of 15:30 (HKT GMT+08:00), China is currently leading the medal count.
