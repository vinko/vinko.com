This evening I saw the movie “[The Mummy – Tomb of the Dragon Emperor](http://www.apple.com/trailers/universal/themummytombofthedragonemperor/ "The The Mummy trailer")” at UA Cinema, Time Square, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong.

Give it a miss even if you don’t have something else better to waste your time on.

I never like the movies that [Brendan Fraser](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000409/) is in, with the latest version of *The Mummy* that further proves my feelings.

I always say how sequels needs to keep the ties between episodes, the worst thing a sequel does is to change the main characters in the story. This episode, they went and change the female lead character, Evelyn Carnahan O’Connell from [Rachel Weisz](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001838/) to [Maria Bello](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0004742/).

Although, they have movie industries big wigs like [Jet Li](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001472/) and [Michelle Yeoh](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000706/).

The short but significant appearance of my friend’s, [Michael Wong](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0939255/) brother [Russell Wong](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0005568/) was a present surprise. Mainly because I did not read the credits before attending the movie.
