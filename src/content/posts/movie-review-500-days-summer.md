This afternoon I saw the movie *[500 Days of Summer](http://www.apple.com/trailers/fox_searchlight/500daysofsummer/ "500 Days of Summer trailer")* at UA Cinema, Times Square, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. Another one of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.html) list.

The movie starts off telling the audience it is not a movie about romance, which further emphasis it as being a movie about love, romance and relationships.

[Joseph Gordon-Levitt](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0330687/ "Joseph Gordon-Levitt profile") in the past was very active in TV series like [*3rd Rock from the Sun*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0115082/ "3rd Rock from the Sun details"), and won awards for his performances. He took time off to attend Columbia University which resulted in his absence from the motion picture industry. This movie is the first leading role in a Hollywood blockbuster since his return from Columbia University. Here Gordon-Levitt is partnered with [Zooey Deschanel](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0221046/ "Zooey Deschanel profile"), who makes a perfect couple, both delivering a believable performance of a relationship that is sometime hot and sometime cold. Something many relationships will go through, hence easy for the audiences to relate.

The supporting casts in this movie were well chosen. With [Geoffrey Arend](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0034309/ "Geoffrey Arend profile") playing Gordon-Levitt’s friend from work, [Chloe Moretz](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm1631269/ "Chloe Moretz profile") playing the much much younger worldly sister, and [Matthew Gray Gubler](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm1693763/ "Matthew Gray Gubler profile") the best friend from Elementary school. All in all the casts, wardrobe and soundtrack gave this movie a homy feel.

Speaking of the music, although the soundtracks are good, for me they did not appear to be the kind of music these two young characters will be into at their age. You can listen to [the soundtrack yourself on iTunes](http://click.linksynergy.com/fs-bin/stat?id=AYDmH5A9AOI&offerid=146261&type=3&subid=0&tmpid=1826&RD_PARM1=http%253A%252F%252Fitunes.apple.com%252FWebObjects%252FMZStore.woa%252Fwa%252FviewAlbum%253Fi%253D324199723%2526id%253D324199526%2526s%253D143441%2526uo%253D6%2526partnerId%253D30 "500 Days of Summer Soundtrack") to decide for yourself. Having said that, the music is the reason that this movie gain an extra penguin in my rating.

The other was the way in which the relationship story was told. It moved back and forth among the 500 days from the first moment Gordon-Levitt’s character met Deschanel’s character; skipping forward several days at a time. If the audiences are not paying attention they may loose track of where in the timeline the story is at.

If you have a very secure relationship I would suggest you watch this well made story of relationship. Otherwise, this may not be a movie for you, but if you watch it you may learn something about your own relationships in the past and why you may not have one.
