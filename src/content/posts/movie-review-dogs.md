This evening I saw the movie *[Old Dogs](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.html "Old Dogs trailer")* at JP Theater, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. One of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.html) list.

With [Seth Green](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001293/ "Seth Green Profile") in this movie I had expected it to be a slapstick comedy. Even with [John Travolta](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000237/ "John Travolta Profile"), who has done great movies: *[Wild Hogs](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0486946/ "Wild Hogs details")*, *[Ladder 49](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0349710/ "Ladder 49 details")*, *[Swordfish](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0244244/ "Swordfish details")*, et., in the past I was a bit skeptical. Fortunately, these are not the only two main actors in the movie, there’s also the Oscar winning [Robin Williams](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000245/ "Robin Williams Profile"), whom I believe have yet to appear is a bad movie; well may be *[RV](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0449089/ "RV details")* was an exception to that rule. At almost 60, Williams is still able to carry the movie on his own with Travolta playing more as a sidekick and supporting actor.

This movie had a fairly simple plot executed well by [Walt Becker](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0065608/ "Walt Becker Profile"). It is about a busy successful man finding the priorities in his life, and pursing them at all cost. Overall there are many touching moments while at the same time contains the humor that is famous in Williams’ films.

This is definitely a must see for Williams fans and anyone else who enjoy a happy fun night out to the movies.
