This evening I saw the movie “[Spider-man 3](http://www.apple.com/trailers/sony_pictures/spider-man_3/)” at JP Theater, Causeway Bay.

I was disappointed. I guess it has to do with “[Tobey Maguire](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001497/)“, he is someone easily hated. Given he plays the hero, Spider-man, of the movie that is not a good thing. Plus, I never thought “[Kirsten Dunst](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000379/)” to be cast correctly as the heroine. I saw them when they were much younger in the first Spider-man, unlike the actors in movie “Harry Potter”, these two did not improve over time.

The supporting actors in the movie were much better, and their humorous scenes saved the movie for me. Many of the kids in the audience were not captured by the actions in the movie or the lack of action. The movie was way too long, at many spots it feels like it was dragging on too long.
