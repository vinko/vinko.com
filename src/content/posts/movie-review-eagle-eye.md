This evening I saw the movie *[Eagle Eye](http://www.apple.com/trailers/paramount/eagleeye/ "Eagle Eye trailer")* at AMC Theater, Pacific Place, Admiralty, Hong Kong.

Another highly anticipated movie from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.php "Must See Movies list") list.

Another great movie with exciting special effects and spectacular stunts. The kind you expects from [Die Hard](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0095016/ "Die Hard details") franchise and more recently in *[Wanted](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0493464/ "Wanted details")*. These recent movies has really raise the bar for all action movies to come.

The casting was almost perfect except for [Michael Chiklis](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0004821/), which I just cannot rid him of his character, “Detective Vic Mackey” from [The Shield](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0286486/ "The Shield detaiils"). It is most likely the choice of the director, [D.J. Caruso](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0142286/ "D.J. Caruso profile"), who chose Chiklis for the Defense Secretary role.

The plot of this movie is not totally original, with [The Terminator](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0088247/) and [WarGames](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0086567/) both using the same theme. To avoid ruin the movie for my readers I will not review too much of the plot, which is reviewed half way into the movie.

Overall the dialogue is fairly weak particularly Chiklis’ character. The only memorable one was from [Billy Bob Thornton](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000671/) where he turns to his team and say, “I hope you are all staring at me because I am the major suspect, if I don’t get some significant leads I will demote everyone of you to jobs that require direct contact of feces.”

If you enjoy action movies and a technology buff, this is definitely a movie not to miss.
