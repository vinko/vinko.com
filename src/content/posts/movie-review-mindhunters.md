This afternoon I went to see the movie “Mindhunters” at Broadway Cinema, Olympian Plaza. Not one of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.php) list.

In a way this is the typical detective story in a way it is not. The one actor (Christian Slater) I thought was the main character actually turns out to be a minor character. The main characters of the story are played by the lesser known actors of Hollywood.

A good mystery overall with the right amount of twists and turns in the plot. If I tell you anymore I will ruin it for you.
