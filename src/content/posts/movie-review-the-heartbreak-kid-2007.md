This evening I saw the movie “[The Heartbreak Kid](http://www.apple.com/trailers/dreamworks/heartbreakkid/)” at Time Square, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong.

[Ben Stiller](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001774/ "Ben Stiller profile") always plays a character in his movies: “[Meet the Parents](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0212338/ "Meet the Parents profile")“, “[Meet the Fockers](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0290002/ "Meet the Fockers profile")“, “[Along Came Polly](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0343135/ "Along Came Polly profile")” and alike. Where his character is the underdog, started out a good nature hero and then forced into situations that makes him appears to be the bad guy, as a result the heroin misunderstands him. This movie was no exception.

So if you are a fan of Ben Stiller, you will not be disappointed. If you’re looking for something different and original, you will need to look elsewhere.
