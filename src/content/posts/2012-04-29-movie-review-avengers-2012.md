This evening I saw the movie *[The Avengers](http://trailers.apple.com/trailers/marvel/avengers/)* at AMC Theater, Pacific Place, Admiralty, Hong Kong. One of the movies from my Must See Movies list, and the following is my movie review.

A highly anticipated movie ever since the appearance of [Robert Downey Jr.](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000375/) with a cameo in *[The Incredible Hulk (2008)](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0800080/)*. After seeing the actual movie I am starting to think this was over hyped.

This motion picture version of The Avengers reminds me so much of movie *[Transformers](https://vinko.squarespace.com/blog/2009/07/01/movie-review-transformers-revenge-of-the-fallen)*. The plot is predictable and slow, the dialog is poor without any chemistry among the characters.

Definitely not a movie worth watching in 3D or IMAX. If you have other options at the box office go with them.
