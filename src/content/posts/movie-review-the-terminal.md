This evening I went to see the movie “[The Terminal](http://www.apple.com/trailers/dreamworks/the_terminal/)” at Broadway Cinema, Olympian City. Another late addition to my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.php) list.

I am again overwhelm by the great acting of Tom Hanks. The character “Viktor Navorski” is incredible. Of course, there may be other factors like, a great script or the famous directions of the director, Steven Spielberg, or the multi million dollar set of the JFK airport.

Yes, they built a set to resemble the International Hall of the JFK airport, and even painted the largest mural as a backdrop for hanging outside the windows of the terminal, just so that it will look believable when one looks out the windows of the terminal.

Tom Hanks has again added another superb performance to his repertoire: “[The Ladykillers](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0335245/)“, “[Catch Me If You Can](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0264464/)“, “[Bands of Brothers](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0185906/)“, “[Cast Away](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0162222/)“, and of course “[Forrest Gump](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0109830/)“.

I will definitely go purchase the DVD for this movie to add to my [collection](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpDVD1.0.4/index.php).
