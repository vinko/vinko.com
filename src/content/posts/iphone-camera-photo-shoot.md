If you think the iPhone camera is not up to par, you may want to think again.

[Lee](http://facebook.com/rlmorrisphoto "Lee Morris Facebook Profile") of [FStoppers.com](http://fstoppers.com/ "F Stoppers.com web site") decided to proof that you can take very beautiful fashion shoot style photos with any camera, even the one from his [iPhone 3GS](http://fstoppers.com/iphone/ "The iPhone Fashion Shoot By Lee Morris"). So he recruited a few friends.
