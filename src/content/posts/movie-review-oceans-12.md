This evening I went to see the movie “[Ocean’s 12](http://www.apple.com/trailers/wb/oceans_12/)” at Broadway Cinema, Olympian Plaza. Another one from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.php) list.

Again I did not go into this sequel having too much expectations like any other sequel. I was expecting the similar twist-and-turn of “[Ocean’s 11](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0240772)“, and to my present surprise, the twists and turns of the story are even more elaborative than one would expect for a sequel.

The characters Danny Ocean (played by George Clooney) and Rusty Ryan (played by Brad Pitt) again had the nicest clothes of all characters.

I really don’t know why Julia Roberts complained about the credit order on the posters. She only played a minor role in this sequel compared to Catherine Zeta-Jones.

From the looks of thing, the writers left room for yet another sequel in case the box office justify so.
