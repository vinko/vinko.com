This evening I saw the movie “[Premonition](http://www.apple.com/trailers/sony_pictures/premonition/)” at UA Windsor Palace, Windsor House, Causeway Bay.

Another movie from one of my favorite actress, [Sandra Bullock](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000113/). She did not disappoint me with this latest movie. She plays a character that no one believe, like “[The Net](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0113957/)” and “[Murder by Numbers](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0264935/)“, and the seriousness as in “[The Lake House](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0410297/)“.

I cannot tell you any more about this movie, otherwise I will give away most of the story.

All I can say is that it reminds me of the movie “[Groundhog Day](http://imdb.com/title/tt0107048/)” with [Bill Murray](http://imdb.com/name/nm0000195/) and the ending is a bit like the ending in “[The Terminator](http://imdb.com/title/tt0088247/)“.
