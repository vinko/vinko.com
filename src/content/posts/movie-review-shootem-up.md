This evening I saw the movie “[Shoot ‘Em Up](http://www.apple.com/trailers/newline/shootemup/)” at JP Theater, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong.

If you like blatant blood and gore you will like this movie. It reminds me of the typical movies [Chow Yun Fat](http://imdb.com/name/nm0000334/) makes in the old days (in Hong Kong).

The story for the movie is typical and the dialogs is bad. They try to give it the [Pulp Fiction](http://imdb.com/title/tt0110912/) style but failed miserably.

So, if you don’t have anything else better to do… I still suggest you not to waste your time with this movie.
