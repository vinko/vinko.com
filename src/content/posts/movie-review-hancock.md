This evening I saw the movie “[Hancock](http://www.apple.com/trailers/sony_pictures/hancock/ "Hancock trailer")” at AMC Theater, Pacific Place, Admiralty, Hong Kong.

Another very good movie by [Will Smith](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000226/). Further proofs that he is a super entertainer: actor, singer and stand-up comedian.

The last movie I watched with Smith was [*The Pursuit of Happiness*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0454921/), where you can read my review of in [my blog](http://blog.vinko.com/2007/03/25/movie-review-the-pursuit-of-happiness/ "Review of The Pursuit of Happiness"), in which he played a totally different character from his typical roles. Like the the previous movie, in Hancock Smith played a down and out character (John Hancock) with super human power that no one likes.

I thought it was very clever of the writers to name the character “John Hancock”. Half way into the movie the plot made a sudden unexpected twist.

I did not recognize [Charlize Theron](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000234/) until the end of the movie when I saw the credits, but I did find the actor very familiar through out the movie. She must have changed her look or matured over time, she no longer have the baby face that she had in past movies.

I am giving it a 4 penguin and you should give it a chance, I am sure you will find it entertaining.
