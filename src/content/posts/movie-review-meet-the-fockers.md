Tonight I went to see the movie “ [Meet the Fockers](http://www.apple.com/trailers/universal/meet_the_fockers/) ” at Broadway Cinema, Olympian Plaza. Another one of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.php) list.

Hilarious… hilarious… hilarious. The more I watch Robert De Niro in comedies (the original, “[Meet the Parents](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0212338/)“, “[Analyze That](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0289848/)“) the more I like him in them. This sequel to the original “[Meet the Parents](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0212338/)” is no different.

Although, the other cast members have something to do with it also. Ben Stiller for one again plays the unlucky guy whom you just can’t help feel sorry for.

So, just watch this and you will have a good night out.
