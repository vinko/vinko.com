This afternoon I went to see the movie “[Shall We Dance](http://click.linksynergy.com/fs-bin/stat?id=AYDmH5A9AOI&offerid=146261&type=3&subid=0&tmpid=1826&RD_PARM1=http%253A%252F%252Fitunes.apple.com%252FWebObjects%252FMZStore.woa%252Fwa%252FviewMovie%253Fid%253D342143425%2526s%253D143441%2526uo%253D6%2526partnerId%253D30)” at Golden Harvest Theater, Ocean Terminal. Another one of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.php) list.

What can I say? I have not seen a bad movie with Richard Gere, and this one is no exception. Every movie that he is in, the director would always guarantee to include a “drop-dead-woman-killer” shot. Like Sean Connery, the older they get the more the women drop at their feet.

This is a nice movie about a save innocent subject matter that makes you feel good afterwards.
