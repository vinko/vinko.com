Here are the photos from the guide (Max). My shots were not bad. It was a bit

challenging to hold the iPhone steady, without a tripod and the sudden gust of wind. I definitely recommend booking a tour, especially the small groups ones. Both our guide and driver were great, very knowledgeable, helpful and caring. They constantly go to different locations to look for clearing in the sky. We were lucky, there were almost no clouds in the sky. Our challenge was look for places that are not too windy. The wind chill is nothing to dismiss on. Wearing layers was the key with a wind proof outer layer
