This evening I saw the movie *[Lady Cop & Papa Crook](http://hk.youtube.com/watch?v=EvkECYTt_MM "Lady Cop & Papa Crook trailer")* (aka《大搜查之女》) at AMC Theater, Pacific Place, Admiralty, Hong Kong. Not one of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.php) list.

I normally do not watch too many Hong Kong made films, particular ones staring pop singers. Tonight it was different, it is a holiday Sunday and I had not watch a film for some weeks. The only US film I was willing to watch, *[Four Christmases](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0369436/ "Four Christmases details")*, was sold out. So I bought tickets for the only Hong Kong made movie that did not seem too boring at the same time may be funny.

Not sure if it was my low expectations, but to my surprise, this movie was definitely entertaining. The two leads: [Sammi Cheng](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0155618/ "Sammi Cheng profile") and [Eason Chan](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0150873/ "Eason Chan profile") were not bad. Best of all were the plot and cinematography. They have this *[Infernal Affairs](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0338564/ "Internal Affairs details")* feel, and the plot twisted also like [*Infernal Affairs*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0338564/ "Internal Affairs details") did.

For those who knows the directors this would not be a surprise, as one of the directors, [Felix Chong](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0159039/ "Felix Chong profile"), is the same director who filmed [*The Departed*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0407887/ "The Departed details"); the 2006 remake of the famous Hong Kong movie [*Infernal Affairs*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0338564/ "Internal Affairs details"). Where the Chong’s partner in those movie, [Alan Mak](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0538320/ "Alan Mak profile"), again teamed up with him to make this movie.

That was two in a row, where a Hong Kong made movie presently surprised me. Next time, even if you have other choices at the cinema, do give this one a check before settling for one of the US films. I for one will not give Hong Kong movies a pass so hastily in the future.
