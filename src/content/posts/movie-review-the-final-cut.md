This evening I went to see the movie “[The Final Cut](http://www.apple.com/trailers/lions_gate/the_final_cut)” at Golden Harvest Theater, Gateway Plaza.

This is not really a typical movie of [Robin Williams](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000245/), but for some reasons this reminds me of his other movie “[One Hour Photo](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0265459/combined)“.

I always believe Robin Williams is one of those great actors of our times, along with Tom Hanks. They are can transform themselves to characters that are not their typical roles. I guess that is another reason why they are great.

Unfortunately, that’s all that is great about this movie. So for the Robin Williams fans, this is another movie for your DVD collection, but no need to rush to see it in the theaters.
