![China Flag](/wp-content/uploads/2010/05/china-flag.jpg)While in Shanghai for the World Expo 2010 the following are sites that I cannot access.

- [My Posterous Site](http://vinko.posterous.com "Vinko's Posterous Site") – although I can send posts to my Posterous site via email, I cannot see how these posts appear on the site as I cannot load it.
- [Facebook](http://facebook.com/vinko "Vinko on Facebook") – I have several comments I like to reply to on my Facebook Wall, but I cannot log into Facebook to do so.
- [Twitter](http://twitter.com/vinko "Vinko on Twitter") – as I had mentioned in my post [Internet Deprived in Shanghai](http://blog.vinko.com/2010/04/30/internet-deprived-shanghai/ "Internet Deprived in Shanghai post"), I cannot access any Twitter services or related sites.
- [FriendFeed](http://friendfeed.com/vinko "Vinko on FriendFeed") – I cannot post anything to FriendFeed because the entire site is block. This may be because it is now own by Facebook.
- [YouTube](http://youtube.com/vinko "Vinko on YouTube") – I cannot see any videos on YouTube referenced by articles/posts I read. Therefore missing much of the content of a post. This lack of access also eliminate my sharing of interesting videos found on YouTube.

Without the above makes it hard for me to share. Fortunately, I still have [Google Buzz](http://google.com/profiles/vinko8 "Vinko's Google Profile") and [Google Reader](feed://www.google.com/reader/public/atom/user%2F10336629614562290951%2Fstate%2Fcom.google%2Fbroadcast "Vinko's Google Reader Feed").
