This afternoon I saw the movie *[Jennifer’s Body](http://www.apple.com/trailers/fox/jennifersbody/ "Jennifer's Body trailer")* at UA Cinema, Times Square, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. Another one of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.html) list.

This is a comedy thriller staring the [*Transformers*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt1369842/ "Transformers details") famed [Megan Fox](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm1083271/ " profile") and [Amanda Seyfried](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm1086543/ "Amanda Seyfried profile") from [*Mamma Mia!*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0795421/ "Mamma Mia details"). It is about a young girl living in a small town that has an unlikely best friend, with very little in common, who ends up being possessed by satan, and starts to kill boys in a high school. As you can tell from the description it is a very Buffy-the-Vampire-Killer like plot.

I believe most male audiences went to the movie because of Fox and most females are only there because their male counter parts ask them to. Fox is not even sexy in this movie.

I would normally remove this movie from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.html) list, but I wanted to make sure this review is accessible as many places as possible.

The only good thing about this movie is the sound track, which I may purchase a few tracks from the iTunes Store. I suggest you rent this movie from iTunes Store when it becomes available even if you are a fan of Fox, hence my 2-penguin rating.
