This evening I saw the movie *[Sherlock Holmes: A Game of Shadows](http://trailers.apple.com/trailers/wb/sherlockholmesagameofshadows/)* at AMC Cinemas, Pacific Place, Admiralty, Hong Kong. One of the movies from my Must See Movies list, and the following is my movie review.

This is the first sequel that I wish there will not be another.

It stars the “always nominated but never wins”, [Robert Downey Jr.](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000375/) and [Jude Law](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000179/). Now with this movie the audiences can clearly see why.

Downey returns to play his ever eccentric and love-thy-self Holmes and Law plays the trusty sidekick who wants to give up on Holmes but can’t bare the thought of doing so. There are so many scenes in the movie that I still cannot figure out the purpose of them being in the movie. Given the movie is center around Holmes’ ultimate rival, [Professor Moriarty](http://www.imdb.com/character/ch0027269/), I find the plot to be too weak, and there are not enough excitement or intrigue to keep the audiences at the edges of their seats.

The movie is so unmemorable that I have already forgotten the ending. Unfortunately, there’s not many movies to choose from during the Holiday breaks, but this is still one to avoid at the theatres.
