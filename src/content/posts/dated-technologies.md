After listening to [Leo Laporte’s](http://leoville.com/) “[The Tech Guy – episode 360](http://www.twit.tv/ttg360)” podcast today. I decided to try and answer the question:

> What technologies we use today, or not long ago, that we may not have tomorrow?

- ![Record player](http://www.vinko.com/images/temp/turntable.jpg)  
  Remember the phonograph, or gramophone? It is also commonly know as “[record player](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Record_player)” or “[turntable](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Record_player)“. It was the common device used to play recorded sounds during the 1870s to 1980s.
- ![Photocopier](http://www.vinko.com/images/temp/photocopier.jpg)  
  The [photocopier](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photocopier) was the must have equipment in an office during the 1980s. Now this has mostly been replaced by digital and analog scanners.
- ![VHS Cassette Tape](http://www.vinko.com/images/temp/vhscassette.jpg)  
  It took almost two decade for the [VHS cassette tape](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vhs) to come out as the winner in the video cassette storage medium war in the 1980s. It won over the arguably superior technology by Sony, [Betamax](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betamax).
- ![Rotary Telephone](http://www.vinko.com/images/temp/rotaryphone.jpg)  
  The [Rotary Dial Telephone](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotary_telephone) was invented by Almon Strowger in 1888 and began to phase out in most major countries by the 1970s.
- ![Traditional Paper Photo Album](http://www.vinko.com/images/temp/photoalbum.jpg)  
  Although the traditionalist still swear by the need of a [classical photo album](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photo_album), this is becoming a less and less desired form of presenting photos to others.
- ![Blu-ray Logo](http://www.vinko.com/images/temp/blu-ray_logo.png)![HD-DVD Logo](http://www.vinko.com/images/temp/hddvd_logo.png)  
  The current [Blu-ray](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blu-ray) and [HD-DVD](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HD_DVD) format war reminisce the [VHS](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vhs) and [Betamax](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betamax) war in the 1980s. Although, this time will the war end before a winner can be decided?

What will be in your list of “technologies we use that we may not have tomorrow”?
