[![Apple Blue Logo](/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/8e621-apple_blue_logo.png)](http://support.apple.com/kb/DL1361 "OS 10.6.7 Combo Updater")Apple made available the maintenance release of Mac OS X version 10.6.7 via their web site and Software Updates.  
You can either use the Software Updates menu item (under the Apple menu) to upgrade your Mac or you can download the [Combo Updater](http://support.apple.com/kb/DL1361 "OS 10.6.7 Combo Updater") from Apple’s Support area.
