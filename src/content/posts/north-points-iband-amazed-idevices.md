![North Point Community Church Logo](/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/northpointchurch-logo.jpg)The North Point Community Church’s “iBand” used apps on their iPad and iPhone to deliver an impressive performance of “*Carol of the Bells*“, “*Rockin’ Around the Christmas Tree*” and “*Feliz Navidad*“.

httpv://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9XNfWNooz4

Although they are not the first to use iOS devices in a performance, they are one of the first to use so many apps at the same time. They even use Apple’s Logic to multitrack the arrangements before they perform the songs.

If you are interested in creating your own iBand, here are the apps they used (source: *[The Ballad of Mr. Fun](http://reidgreven.wordpress.com/2010/12/07/north-point-iband-apps/)*).

#### Carol of the Bells

- Eddie Kirkland – [SoundGrid](http://blog.vinko.com/link/soundgrid/)
- Reid Greven – [NLogFree](http://blog.vinko.com/link/nlog/)1
- Jared Hamilton – [Melody Bell](http://blog.vinko.com/link/melody-bell/)
- Danny Grady – [Guitarist](http://blog.vinko.com/link/guitarist/) (Nylon String)
- Antwane McMullin, drums – [iGog](http://blog.vinko.com/link/igog/) (MoreVox Acoustic 1 sound)
- Rick Meeder, bass – [Bassist](http://blog.vinko.com/link/bassist/)
- Steve Marcia – [Guitarist](http://blog.vinko.com/link/guitarist/) (Electric)

#### Rockin’ Around the Christmas Tree

- Antwane McMullin, drums – [iGog](http://blog.vinko.com/link/igog/)
- Rick Meeder, bass – [Bassist](http://blog.vinko.com/link/bassist/)
- Danny Grady, electric guitar on intro & fills – [Guitarist](http://blog.vinko.com/link/guitarist/)
- Steve Marcia, acoustic guitar on main melody – [Guitarist](http://blog.vinko.com/link/guitarist/)
- Jared Hamilton, B3 – [Pocket Organ C3B3](http://blog.vinko.com/link/pocket-organ/)
- Reid Greven, sax solo – [Saxophone Musicofx](http://blog.vinko.com/link/saxophone-musicofx/)
- Eddie Kirkland, Percussions – [Tambourine!](http://blog.vinko.com/link/tambourine/)

#### Feliz Navidad

- Joe Lee, guiro – [Percussions](http://blog.vinko.com/link/percussions/)
- Antwane McMullin, drums – [iGog](http://blog.vinko.com/link/igog/) (Rock Kit 1 sound)
- Rick Meeder, bass – [Bassist](http://blog.vinko.com/link/bassist/)
- Danny Grady, nylon string guitar – [Guitarist](http://blog.vinko.com/link/guitarist/)
- Ben Snider, clave – [Percussions](http://blog.vinko.com/link/percussions/)
- Steve Marcia, maracas & tambourine – [Percussions](http://blog.vinko.com/link/percussions/)
- Jared Hamilton, melody solo & percussion fills – [Bebot](http://blog.vinko.com/link/bebot/), [Percussions](http://blog.vinko.com/link/percussions/)
- Eddie Kirkland, congas & bongos – [Percussions](http://blog.vinko.com/link/percussions/)
- Reid Greven, piano – [Pianist](http://blog.vinko.com/link/pianist/)2
- Seth Condrey, vocals – T-Pain

1 NLog has an universal version native to the iPad called [NLogSynth Pro](http://blog.vinko.com/link/nlogsynth-pro/).  
2 Precussions has an universal version native to the iPad called [Precussions+](http://blog.vinko.com/link/precussions+/).
