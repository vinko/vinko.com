This afternoon I saw the movie *[Iron Man 2](http://www.apple.com/trailers/paramount/ironman/ "Iron Man 2 trailer")* at AMC Cinemas, Pacific Place, Admiralty, Hong Kong. One of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/mustsee)

Many people criticized the movie as not being as good as the previous episode in the sequel, *[Iron Man](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0371746/ "Iron Man details")*. These people did not like the “[Tony Stark](http://www.imdb.com/character/ch0020749/ "Tony Stark profile")” character played by [Robert Downey Jr.](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000375/ "Robert Downey Jr. profile") for trying too hard to be funny. Nor did they like the fact that the casting directors: [Sarah Finn](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0278168/ "Sarah Finn profile") and [Randi Hiller](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0384900/ "Randi Hiller profile"), changed the actor who played “[Lt. Col. James ‘Rhodey’ Rhodes](http://www.imdb.com/character/ch0033332/ "Lt. Col. James Rhodes profile")” from [Terrence Howard](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0005024/ "Terrence Howard Profile") to [Don Cheadle](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000332/ "Don Cheadle profile"). The decision might had been from Howard himself, as he was starting in a new movie *[Red Tails](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0485985/ "Red Tails details")*.

I personally liked the Tony Stark that Downey portrayed. A bit egocentric, a bit arrogant and dare devil. Something that Downey plays very well as he did in *[Sherlock Holmes](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0988045/ "Sherlock Holmes details")* and *[Kiss Kiss Bang Bang](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0373469/ "Kiss Kiss Bang Bang details")*.

Someone who stood out in this movie was [Mickey Rourke](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000620/ "Mickey Rourke profile") who played a great villain, as he did in *The Informers*, *[Domino](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0421054/ "Domino details")*, *[Sin City](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0401792/ "Sin City details"),* [Rumble Fish](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0086216/ "Rumble Fish details") and many more. I just wished his character did not die so easily.

The story of this movie was a lot weaker compared to the first movie, may be this was due to the fact that the audience no longer had to be told the Iron Man story. The writers did try to introduce some moments of reminiscing for Tony Stark’s childhood, but the plot was too weak and not captivating. The reintroduction of [Samuel L. Jackson](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000168/ "Samuel L. Jackson profile")‘s character “[Nick Fury](http://www.imdb.com/character/ch0020752/ "Nick Fury profile")” and Clark Gregg’s character “[Agent Coulson](http://www.imdb.com/character/ch0037737/ "Agent Coulson profile")” felt half done and kept the audience wanting more. For those who did not see the first Iron Man movie will had no idea where these people came from and why they were helping Tony Stark.

Not to mention unbelievable character “[Natalie Rushman](http://www.imdb.com/character/ch0020745/ "Natalie Rushman profile") / [Natasha Romanoff](http://www.imdb.com/character/ch0020745/ "Natalie Rushman profile")” played by Scarlett Johansson. Natalie reminds me of the other heroine “[Æon Flux](http://www.imdb.com/character/ch0006966/ "Æon Flux profile")” from [the movie](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0402022/ "Æon Flux details") of the same name. May be Marvel was planning another action movie with the “Natalie” character, like they gave Tony Stark a guest appearance in *[The Incredible Hulk (2008)](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0800080/ "The Incredible Hulk details")*.

After watching the movie I felt the writers and editors were trying to keep the movie within 2 hours I think this movie was entertaining for an afternoon out, but it lacked the interesting twists in the plot to justify the extra “penguin” in my rating.
