---
title: "Finally no GST in HK"
description: "At least for the time being."
pubDate: 2006-12-05
---

At least for the time being.

I guess [my efforts](http://www.petitiononline.com/saynogst/) to stop the Hong Kong government’s decision to impose a GST in Hong Kong had not been in vain.

The Hong Kong government announced today that they will, for the time being, put off the discussion of whether to impose a GST in Hong Kong.
