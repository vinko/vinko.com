This afternoon I went to see the movie “[National Treasure](http://www.apple.com/trailers/disney/national_treasure/)” at Broadway Cinema, Olympian Plaza. Another one of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.php) list.

What can I say… yet another great movie.

For fans of “[Indiana Jones](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0097576)” and “[Lara Croft](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0146316)” series of movies, this is another to add to that collection. May be “Ben Gates” will be another name to remember.

There are so many elements of the other series, including the constant revealing of clues and chases by the bad guys of the hero.

The writers definitely set this movie up for potential sequel.
