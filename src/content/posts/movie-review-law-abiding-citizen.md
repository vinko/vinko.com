This afternoon I saw the movie *[Law Abiding Citizen](http://www.apple.com/trailers/independent/lawabidingcitizen/ "2012 trailer")* at AMC Theater, Admiralty, Hong Kong. Another one of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.html) list.

Most surprising part of this movie is the role of [Gerard Butler](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0124930/ "Gerard Butler profile") who is most known for [*Beowulf*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0402057/ "Beowulf details") and [*300*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0416449/ "300 details"). In this movie Butler began as a victim who had his family murdered in front of him and then getting even after the justice system failed to convict the ones responsible. So far this does not sound too unusual but the way Butler’s character got even and the character’s background made the movie interesting for me, giving it a feel of [MacGyver](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacGyver "MacGyver in Wikipedia").

If you like [*The Bourne Identity*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0258463/ "The Bourne Identity details") movie franchise you will like this movie.
