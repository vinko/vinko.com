This evening I saw the movie “[A Lot Like Love](http://imdb.com/title/tt0391304/)” on HBO Asia.

Normally I do not do reviews of movies on HBO, that’s because I normally would had already seen them in theaters and subsequently did a review of it.

In this case, the movie was never shown in Hong Kong theaters when it was first released in the US April 22, 2005.

This is a romantic comedy starting [Ashton Kutcher](http://imdb.com/name/nm0005110/) (of “That ’70s Show” TV series which I hated, and more recently “[The Guardian](http://www.vinko.com/weblog/2006/11/movie-review-guardian.shtml)“) and [Amanda Peet](http://imdb.com/name/nm0001605/).

May be it si Kutcher’s 70s Show image that I always have in mind, I cannot see him in a series role, through out the movie I was expecting him to do something stupid just for a laugh. I must say I was presently surprise that he brought the right amount of humore to the movie.

So if you have not seen it or do not have HBO, definitely get it from iTunes Store or your neighbourhood HMV.
