Tonight I went to see the movie “[Star Wars: Episode III – Revenge of the Sith](http://www.starwars.com/explore/the-movies/episode-iii/)” at Ocean Terminal.

Although, this is yet another sequel of the many in the past few months. It is a must see for all Star Wars fans.

For the past 28 years many of us had lived with the story of Star Wars. For those who did not read the books, we were left with a set of untold stories from the original Star Wars movie back in 1977. Now with this latest version of the Star Wars saga, we are finally able to visually satisfy the numerous questions left after watching the first three epsiodes (IV, V, VI) of the series over 20 years ago.

George Lucas is definitely a perfectionist and amazing story teller. With this latest movie, he has closed the book on the story that he started 28 years ago. Every aspects of the story were completed nicely. Particularly the ending that lead smoothly into the original movie (now known as “Star Wars: Episode IV – A New Hope”).

He even included battle scenes that he and his team made famous with the original 3 movies in the series back in the 80’s.

I did not give it a perfect score only because, for some reason, it still did not have the spark that I experienced with the original 3 movies.
