Yesterday many media [reported](http://mashable.com/2010/03/24/diggs-iphone-app-is-now-available/) that the ever popular social news aggregation site, [Digg.com](http://digg.com) is releasing an iPhone app.

This evening the app appeared in Apple’s iTunes App Stores for UK, HK and several others, but not the iTunes App Store in the US. Very strange indeed.

So if you’re in one of the lucky countries do [check it](http://click.linksynergy.com/fs-bin/stat?id=AYDmH5A9AOI&offerid=146261&type=3&subid=0&tmpid=1826&RD_PARM1=http%253A%252F%252Fitunes.apple.com%252Fgb%252Fapp%252Fdigg%252Fid362872995%253Fmt%253D8%2526uo%253D6%2526partnerId%253D30) out and leave comments here of your thoughts. If you live in the US then you will have to wait a bit longer for the app.
