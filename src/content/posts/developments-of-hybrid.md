Recently I notice both Toyota and its Lexus brand have made hybrids. These are not the typical hippie boring family car, but the [Highlander and Camry models](http://www.toyota.com/index.html "Toyota Models") for Toyota and [GS and RX models](http://www.lexus.com/models/allModels/ "Lexus Models") for Lexus. Honda did not stand still either, they have hybrid models of their [Civic models](http://automobiles.honda.com/civic-hybrid/ "Civic Models"). There are other American manufactures who also produces hybrid models. Luxury manufactures like: Mercedes and Porsche also have respective hybrid models at a even higher premium.

Even these modern alternatives do not compare to the offerings from [Tesla Motors](http://www.teslamotors.com/ "Tesla Motors web site"), a 100% electric car, that produce performance of 0 – 60 mph in less than 4 seconds, while still offer a 135-mpg equivalent efficiency. Although the price tag is a whopping USD98K for the base model.

![Audi A1 Sportback Hybrid (Concept)](http://www.gizmag.com/pictures/hero/10192_15100812544.jpg)
*Audi A1 Sportback Hybrid (Concept)*

Now manufactures like Audi is one upping its Q7 Hybrid, by introducing the [A1 Sportback Hybrid](http://www.gizmag.com/audi-a1-hybrid-drive-sportback-concept/10192/ "GizMag Article on Audi A1 Sportback Hybrid") concept. All this is very exciting to see, making the choice of purchasing a hybrid is no longer a decision that requires compromising on esthetics. Since the environment really needs us to do our part.
