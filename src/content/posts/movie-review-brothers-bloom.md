This evening I saw the movie  *[Brothers Bloom](http://www.apple.com/trailers/paramount/gijoeriseofcobra/ "Brothers Bloom trailer")* at AMC Theater, Pacific Place, Admiralty, Hong Kong. Another one of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.html) list.

It has been a while since a movie offered a surprising plot. I was glad to say that this one did and that was the reason for one of the penguins above.

[Adrien Brody](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0004778/ "Adrien Brody Profile") gave a very good performance as “Bloom”, but I see the same character he played in his famous role as “Wladyslaw Szpilman” in the movie *[The Pianist](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0253474/ "The Pianist details")*. On the other hand [Mark Ruffalo](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0749263/ "Mark Ruffalo Profile") gave a typical B-movie role as he always did in the past, even though, Ruffalo was suppose to be a co-star in the movie. [Rachel Weisz](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001838/ "Rachel Weisz profile"), the other lead in this movie had a pretty good performance, unlike the other movies she appeared in like, *[The Mummy Returns](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0209163/ "The Mummy Returns details").*

A surprise performance came from [Rinko Kikuchi](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0452860/ "Rinko Kikuchi profile") who played “Bang Bang”. This was Kikuchi’s second American movie since *[Babel](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0452860/ "Babel details")* in 2006. I had not seen any of Kikuchi’s Japanese movies but Babel was pretty bad. I think her English is not that good, which was perfect for the “Bang Bang” character, as she did not speak much, makingher acting even more important to the role.

The other thing that earned a penguin in my rating was the wardrobe. Both Brody and Weisz had beautiful clothes, which reminds me why I love Winter weather clothing.

I did not have this movie on my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.html) list before I saw the movie and I’m glad to have added it to the list. I think if you were like me and saw all that was at the theater, you may want to give this one a try.
