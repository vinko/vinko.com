This afternoon I went to see the movie “[Man on Fire](http://www.apple.com/trailers/fox/man_on_fire/final_trailer/)” at AMC, Festival Walk. Another one of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.php) list.

This is a remake of the original movie back in [1987](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0093489/), staring [Scott Glenn](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001277/). Majority of the movie had me capsulated but unfortunately I was still distracted by celluar phones going off.

Denzel Washington did a very good job, playing a character which he played many times before in other movies.

The ending is a bit disappointing and unlike the 1987 version. If I tell you any more I will give away the plot.
