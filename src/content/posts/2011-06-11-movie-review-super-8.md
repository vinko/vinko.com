This evening I saw the movie *[Super 8](http://trailers.apple.com/trailers/paramount/super8/)* at AMC Cinemas, Pacific Place, Admiralty, Hong Kong. One of the movies from my Must See Movies list, and the following is my movie review.

Wow… this movie got right into it at the start. We only have to wait about 15 minutes to receive a spectacular train crash, then kept a steady pace throughout. It has the spirit and style of [E.T.](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0083866/) and [Close Encounter of the Third Kind](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0075860/) all over it. Even though [Steven Spielberg](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000229/) only receives the “producer’ credits we can see much of his influences in the movie.

On the other hand the director, writer and producer, [J.J. Abrams](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0009190/) is famous for his hands in the hit TV series: [Alias](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0285333/), [Lost](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0411008/) and [Fringe](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt1119644/). Abrams also has some motion picture to his credits including some of the biggest box office winners: *[Mission: Impossible III](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0317919/)* and *[Star Trek](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0796366/)*, but it also includes some of the busts like *[Cloverfield](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt1060277/)*. The latter is what cause me to be weary about purchasing the tickets, but I believe Spielberg will not associate himself with anything bad which reassures me.

Like Spielberg’s E.T. that launched the careers of [Drew Barrymore](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000106/) and [Henry Thomas](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001794/), this movie also introduces two new child actors: [Joel Courtney](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm1525807/) and [Riley Griffiths](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm4087524/), of whom did a much better job than the so call veterans in the movie: [Ron Eldard](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0253035/) and [Kyle Chandler](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0151419/).

We should watch Courtney’s development to see if he develops into the star that Barrymore did minus the drug addictions or follows the paths of Thomas. Courtney portrays a kid who recently lost his mother and has a great passion for makeup and model making. He practices his passion by helping his kindergarten friend, played by Griffiths, with a no budget movie. One night while filming bought them close encounter with a major government cover up.

This is definitely a must see for 2011 and for fans of children adventures and E.T. aficionados.
