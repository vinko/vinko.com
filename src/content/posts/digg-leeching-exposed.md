With the proliferation of “Social Bookmarks Managers” like [Furl](http://www.furl.net), [Del.icio.us](http://del.icio.us) and [Digg.Com](http://www.digg.com) it is unfortunate that people like those described in this [article](http://thetechnologist.blogspot.com/2005/08/digg-leeching-exposed.html) (by TheTechnologist) would exploit (“leech”) these managers for their own monetary gains.

I think owners and operators of these “Social Bookmarks Managers” should do something to stop these leechers, while still allowing legitimate users like ourselves to blog and link to Digg stores.

PS: I urge readers of this article by TheTechnologist NOT to click on the “leechers'” links.
