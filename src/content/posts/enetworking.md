Yesterday I was introduced to a web site for business networking. It is called “[LinkedIn.Com](http://www.linkedin.com)“, it is essentially a personal network like “[Friendster.Com](http://www.friendster.com)” and alike. The difference is LinkedIn requires referrals from individuals in the immediate network level, before one can contact someone else on subsequent network levels. Therefore, it is very similar to real life business networking, providing the necessary privacy one requires.
