SeeReal, a German company, developed a high resolution Holographic Display that allows you to see 3D images with a LCD flat panel display.

This is the kind of display they extrapolated along with the Heliodisplay, by IO2Technology, in the 2003 movie “Paycheck” staring Ben Affleck.
