Back in April I wrote and article in my [Blog](http://www.vinko.com/weblog/blogger.shtml "Vinko's Blog") titled, “[Made Up Words](http://www.vinko.com/weblog/2006/04/made-up-words.shtml "Made Up Words")“. Now Webster dictionary have added the word “google” into their 11th edition of the [Webster dictionary](http://www.m-w.com/dictionary/google "Webster on Goolge"). Other new words include: “[mouse potato](http://www.m-w.com/dictionary/mouse%20potato "Webster on Mouse Potato") and [spyware](http://www.m-w.com/dictionary/spyware "Webster on Sypware").
