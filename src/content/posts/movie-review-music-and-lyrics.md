This evening I saw the movie “[Music and Lyrics](http://www.apple.com/trailers/wb/musicandlyrics/)” at UA Cinema, Time Square, Causeway Bay

Impressive performance by [Drew Barrymore](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000106/). She sure have come a long way since her performance in [Steven Spielberg’s](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000229/) “[E.T.”](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0083866/) and her come back movie “[Charlie’s Angels](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0160127/).

On the other hand [Hugh Grant’s](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000424/) performance is nothing special. He plays his typical British school boy character that he does in every movie he appears in.

All in all this was a movie designed to be released during Valentine’s Day. Although entertaining I should not have paid to see it at a theater.
