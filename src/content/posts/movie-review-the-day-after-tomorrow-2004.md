Tonight I watched “The Day After Tomorrow” at Olympian City Broadway.

The movie definitely had lots of CGI, but the story was simple and predictable, the acting was so so and the characters were not developed well enough for me. The movie did not grab or maintained my attention for the 2 hours plus that it lasted.
