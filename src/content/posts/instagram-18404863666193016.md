@theclublounges is the only lounge at this part of , other than lounge. It has partnerships with many including @StarluxAirlines, as a result becomes extremely busy. It got to a point where passengers were waiting outside.

The lounge has very limited charging solutions. Most power outlets appear to be situated for vacuuming rather than passenger use, as they are far away from seats. There are some outlets on a short bar counter, you can imagine this is where majority of the passengers gathered.

Food selection is very limited. Typical for a North American airline lounge. Fortunately, I don’t have to pay to get in here. Not worth it!
