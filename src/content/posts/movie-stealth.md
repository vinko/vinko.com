This evening I went to see the movie “[Stealth](http://www.apple.com/trailers/sony_pictures/stealth/)” at Broadway Theater, Olympian City Plaza.

What can I say, I should have known from the cast in this movie and the numerous bad press I heard from its theatrical release in the United States. Although, may be it is because of these low expectation going into the movie that I actually enjoyed it. Or it could be because I did not have to pay for it; it was on Broadway Theater, from completing a customer service survey this past Sunday.

So, if you like me and do not have to pay for this movie, and not have much of an expectation, you may too enjoy it.
