A few days ago I mentioned in my post, [Things to do Before Upgrading iPhone to 3.1.x](http://blog.vinko.com/2009/09/13/upgrading-iphone-31x/), if you do not do what described then you will not be able to jailbreak or unlock ever again, or until the hacking community figure out a way.

Well there is now a way. If you had stored your 3.0 iBSS and iBEC files on the Cydia server. If that is not the case, you will want to follow [the instructions](http://www.saurik.com/id/12 "Instructions for connecting to on-file feature") at Jay Freeman’s web site.

Now that the iPhone Dev Team has released [Pwnage Tool 3.1](http://blog.iphone-dev.org/post/188779017/3-o-fun "Pwnage Tool 3.1") for iPhone, iPhone 3G and iPod Touch 1G. The version for the iPhone 3GS, iPod Touch 2G and 3G will be coming soon.

As always I do not see a need for jailbreaking at the moment, and if you do not have to, I do recommend you seek someone who has the technical background to understand that process for assistance, especially if you do not have the skills yourself.
