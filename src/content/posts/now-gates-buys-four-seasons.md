Will this be another way for Billy to impose Microsoft technology on an industry that has yet to embrace technology with full open arms?

I sure hope not. As Four Seasons is one of the more prestigious hotel chains in the world, and gaining the status of the largest hotel (as of 2006) in Hong Kong when it opened its doors in 2006 with 1000 hotel rooms.

May be Gates is hoping to use the rooms of the Four Seasons chain as experimental grounds for Microsoft’s next attempt to enter the “future” homes.
