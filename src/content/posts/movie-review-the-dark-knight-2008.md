This evening I saw the movie “[The Dark Knight](http://www.apple.com/trailers/wb/thedarkknight/ "The Dark Knight trailer")” at AMC Theatre, Pacific Place, Admiralty, Hong Kong.

Another great movie! That’s two in a row, two weeks ago was [*Hancock*](http://blog.vinko.com/2008/07/06/movie-review-hancock/ "Review of Hancock") and two weeks before that was [*Wanted*](http://blog.vinko.com/2008/06/27/movie-review-wanted-2008/ "Review of Hancock"). This is the best Batman movie I have seen thus far.

There was enough continuation from the last Batman movie, *[Batman Begins](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0372784/ "Batman Begins info.")*. Just the appropriate amount of draw from the plot of the previous movie in the sequel. Definitely, the best looking Batman outfit of them all.

The one thing I did not like about the movie, was the directory or set decoration to hide the city where they filmed Gotham City. In the past Gotham City has always been this make believe or futuristic city where a super hero like Batman would appear.

One of the best part of the movie, like in *[Batman (1989)](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0096895/ "Batman info.")*, was the character [The Joker](http://www.imdb.com/character/ch0000180/ "Info. on The Joker"). In the 1989 version of Batman, The Joker was played by the amazing [Jack Nicholson](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000197/), this time it was played by the late, [Heath Ledger](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0005132/ "Info. on Heath Ledger") of [Brokeback Mountain](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0388795/) fame.

Another thing this Batman movie tries to do, is to explain the origin of [Harvey Dent](http://www.imdb.com/character/ch0000190/ "Info. on the character Harvey Dent") (aka. Two-Face), this time played by [Aaron Eckhart](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001173/), this character first appeared in the 1989 version of *Batman*, played by [Billy Dee Williams](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001850/) then again in [*Batman Forever*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0112462/ "Batman Forever info.") by [Tommy Lee Jones](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000169/). As a result it is a good sequel to *Batman Begins*.

All I need to do is collect these Batman movies since 1989 and put them back in chronological order, I will have a complete picture of how each of the villains came about.

If you are a Batman fan, this is definitely a must see. If you are not, still go see it, you will not be disappointed.
