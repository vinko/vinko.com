This evening I saw the movie “[Jumper](http://www.apple.com/trailers/fox/jumper/ "Jumper trailer")” at Time Square, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong.

This movie has the feel of the Bourne series. This is not surprising, since it is also directed by the same director, [Doug Liman](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0510731/ "Doug Liman's profile") as the Bourne series: “[The Bourne Identity](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0258463/ "The Bourne Identity profile")“, “[The Bourne Supremacy](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0372183/ "The Bourne Supremacy profile")” and “[The Bourne Ultimatum](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0440963/ "The Bourne Ultimatum profile")“.

You really need to walk into the movie with an open mind and just enjoy the movie as the director has present it. Most of all you should not be analyzing or questioning the possibility of what is portrayed. Fortunately, I did that, hence the 4 penguins, otherwise I might have given it a lower rating.

The acting was so so, I never liked the acting of [Hayden Christensen](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0159789/ "Hayden Christensen profile") and this movie is no exception.
