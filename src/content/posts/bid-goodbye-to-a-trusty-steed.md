Today I closed another chapter in my atuo-ownership history. My [2001 TT Quattro Roadster](http://flickr.com/photos/vinkotsui/sets/72157600322898411/) received a new owner.

I bid goodbye to it after 37100 km and 21 months of enjoyment.

[![My Audi TT](http://farm2.static.flickr.com/1366/534292967_23865c9423_m.jpg)](http://farm2.static.flickr.com/1366/534292967_9a3964128c_o.jpg)

Many memories were had in this car. Fortunately, I have some [photos](http://flickr.com/photos/vinkotsui/sets/72157600322898411/) to remember it by. The unfortunate part is that I only have the one photo below showing me enjoying it.

[![Me in Audi TT](http://farm2.static.flickr.com/1279/673608825_4e177fad3b_m.jpg)](http://flickr.com/photos/vinkotsui/sets/72157600322898411/)

I hope you are as anxious as I am to find out what the next chapter in my auto-ownership will be…
