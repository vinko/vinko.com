Finally the highly anticipated last book of the Harry Potter series has a publishing date, July 21, 2007, 01:00 BST worldwide (English speaking countries). It also has a final name, “Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows”.

I guess I will have to begin Book #5: “Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix” again for the 5th time, then start and finish Book#6, “Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince” in the next few months.

Especially when the motion picture “Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix” will be released July 13, 2007 worldwide.
