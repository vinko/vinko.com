This evening I saw the movie *[Bangkok Dangerous](http://www.apple.com/trailers/lions_gate/bangkokdangerous/ "Bangkok Dangerous trailer")* at AMC Theater, Pacific Place, Admiralty, Hong Kong.

Not one of [Nicolas Cage](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000115/)‘s better movies. He has some fairly bad ones lately, like *[Ghost Rider](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0259324/)*, *[The Wicker Man](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0450345/)* and *[The Weather Man](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0384680/)* just to name a few. Cage really shows his age in this movie and the type of characters he normally plays in movies like [National Treasure](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0465234/) are not longer believable.

There are really no one who can do it like [Sean Connery](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000125/) and still plays an action hero well into his late 50s. Actors like Nicolas Cage should be more careful in choosing characters where he can play a more believable character.

You will come out of this movie feeling unfulfilled and cheated of 100 minutes of your time. So, if you are a fan of Nicolas Cage try to rent it from iTunes when it becomes available, otherwise choose  something else.
