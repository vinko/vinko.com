Two days ago, the growing popular software “[Skype](http://www.skype.com/intl/en/get-skype/)“; by the company of the same name, finally released a Beta version for the Macintosh (OS 10.3.5 and later). This is after only 9 months where our friends on the [Windows](http://www.skype.com/intl/en/get-skype/), [Linux](http://www.skype.com/intl/en/get-skype/) and [Pocket PC](http://www.skype.com/intl/en/get-skype/) platforms have been enjoying the features and functionalities of VoIP with Skype.

The creators of Skype are the same ones who created one of the most popular Internet software “[KaZaA](http://www.kazaa.com)“; a P2P software that people used to exchange documents, MP3, movies and images.

The [Skype](http://www.skype.com) team further enhanced what they learned from KaZaA and introduced a bunch of other technologies to produce a VoIP client that automatically locate the shortest path between Skype users.

They also have a service called SkypeOut where you can call any telephone, either fixed line or mobile, at a very low cost.

So if you have time and a microphone attached to your computer do give this software a try, while you’re at it you can drop me a line (Skype ID: “vinko66”).
