Today I watched the movie [The Ladykillers](http://www.apple.com/trailers/touchstone/the_ladykillers/index.html) at UA TimeSquare in Causeway Bay. Another late addition to my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.php) list.

Tom Hanks again delivered a superb acting of a sly Southern American. His character was very believable and mesmerizing.

Although, the story is simple and some what predictable, the movie was very entertaining due to the great acting by all.
