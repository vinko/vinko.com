This evening I went to see the movie “[Firewall](http://www.apple.com/trailers/wb/firewall/hd/)” at UA Cinema, Time Square.

Harrison Ford played his typical role of the underdog honest guy, as he did in “[Clear and Present Danger](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0109444/)“, “[Air Force One](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0118571/)“, “[Patriot Games](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0105112/)“, “[The Fugitive](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0106977/)” and “[Frantic](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0095174/)“. Of which I’ve seen them all.

If it was not for the twist and turn of Firewall; again typical of this series of Ford’s work, one would be bored with the movie.

For some reason, I for one, cannot get enough of Ford’s series: “Indiana Jones” series, “Jack Ryan” series and “Star Wars” series.

Although I am a fan I think this one can wait for the DVD with this one.
