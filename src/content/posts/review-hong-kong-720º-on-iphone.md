![Hong Kong 720º Logo](/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/hk-720-logo.jpg)Today Thomas B. ([@thomashk](http://twitter.com/thomashk/ "Thomas on Twitter")) introduced me to an iPhone application, [Hong Kong 720º](http://click.linksynergy.com/fs-bin/stat?id=AYDmH5A9AOI&offerid=146261&type=3&subid=0&tmpid=1826&RD_PARM1=http%253A%252F%252Fitunes.apple.com%252FWebObjects%252FMZStore.woa%252Fwa%252FviewSoftware%253Fid%253D310541606%2526mt%253D8%2526uo%253D6%2526partnerId%253D30 "Hong Kong 720º in iTunes App Store"), released by the [Hong Kong Tourism Board](http://www.discoverhongkong.com "HKTB web site") (HKTB). It is designed to promote Hong Kong attractions while assisting visitors with locating famous tourist spots, and getting a virtual tour of the attractions before they arrive to help plan their visits to Hong Kong. Best of all this application is available in the [iTunes App Store for free](http://click.linksynergy.com/fs-bin/stat?id=AYDmH5A9AOI&offerid=146261&type=3&subid=0&tmpid=1826&RD_PARM1=http%253A%252F%252Fitunes.apple.com%252FWebObjects%252FMZStore.woa%252Fwa%252FviewSoftware%253Fid%253D310541606%2526mt%253D8%2526uo%253D6%2526partnerId%253D30 "Hong Kong 720º in iTunes App Store").

The application contains QuickTime VR tours of all the major tourist locations, including Google Map of the location, English and Chinese phrase for the tourist to show taxi drivers or others when they are lost, recommended travel directions, and photo gallery of the tourist attraction.

Additional to tourist attraction information, it also contains tourist promotional videos of Hong Kong, locations of the Visitor Centers around the city, important telephone numbers for any travelers.

It is very encouraging to see an Asian government body adapt new technology to promote a location to the world.

This application was released by HKTB back in May 31, 2009, and since then there were 2 updates. Unfortunately, I cannot find any mention of this application on the HKTB web site, and none of the first two result pages from a Google search included pages from [HKTB web site](http://www.discoverhongkong.com "HKTB web site"). When I went directly to HKTB web site the only page the mention the iPhone resulted is [a 404 Error](http://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/interactive/iphone.html "HKTB iPhone Interactive application") (page not found).

On one hand it is good to see that HKTB spent the effort (and money) to create the application, but it is unfortunate, they they did not promote the application; not even on their own web site.
