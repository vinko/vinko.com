I have been craving for Tonkatsu recently, so I took the opportunity to visit one of Hong Kong’s best @tonkichitonkatsuseafood .

I ordered the medium pork loin with giant prawns; they were indeed giants. With the unlimited refills of cabbage and rice, this is definitely enough for two. The pork were juicy but lean with just the right amount of fat and the prawns were fresh. Neither tasted oily given they were all deep fried.

No worries about eating all these fried food, they offer every table a Japanese tea to help digest the fried food. Also a bit of watermelon to help at the end of the meal.

Almost forgot to mention, service is very efficient.

If you’re looking for Tonkatsu definitely need to give this place a try.
