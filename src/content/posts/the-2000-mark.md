Another month another milestone has been reached for my online [store](http://stores.ebay.com/vinkostreasures). Today I reached 2000 feedback points on eBay while still maintaining a 100% rating. According to most, maintaining a “100% rating” with such high number of feedbacks (2186) is a rare achivement on eBay.

As you will see from the actual [comments](http://feedback.ebay.com/ws/eBayISAPI.dll?ViewFeedback&userid=vinko8&ssPageName=STRK:ME:UFS) of past customers, much of these feedbacks had been the result of the customer service delivered.

This further proofs that a customer focused business would be more successful than simply selling to make money. If I just focused on delivering the best quality products at the most competitive price, without also providing the excellent customer service I would not have the high percentage (30%) of return customers and referrals.
