This evening I saw the movie “[Fracture](http://www.apple.com/trailers/newline/fracture/)” at UA Cinema, Time Square, Causeway Bay.

Another movie staring [Anthony Hopkins](http://imdb.com/name/nm0000164/). He plays a character similar to his “Dr. Hannibal Lecter” character from the Hannibal series. An intelligent, devious and meticulous character.

The similarity of this movie with the Hannibal series is not a coincident. As the screenplay is again written by [Thomas Harris](http://imdb.com/name/nm0365383/), the author of “[Hannibal Rising](http://imdb.com/title/tt0367959/)“, “[Red Dragon](http://imdb.com/title/tt0289765/)“, “[Hannibal](http://imdb.com/title/tt0212985/)” and “[The Silence of the Lambs](http://imdb.com/title/tt0102926/)“.

The surprise for my in this movie; aside from the plot, is the performance of “[Ryan Gosling](http://imdb.com/name/nm0331516/)“. He fits the character, “Willy Beachum”, perfectly. Who is a young up and coming prosecutor for the city of Los Angeles.

I cannot say any more about the movie or I will ruin it for you. If you like a crime mystery you will definitely like this movie.
