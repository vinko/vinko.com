My Rating:

This evening I saw the movie *[The Other Woman (aka Love and Other Impossible Pursuits)](http://trailers.apple.com/trailers/fox/xmenfirstclass/)* at UA Cinemas, Times Square, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. One of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/mustsee) list.

This movies stars the Academy Awards Best Actress, [Natalie Portman](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000204/). It was filmed in 2009 and general released early 2011, I wonder if this has anything to do with Portman’s Best Actress win in 2011.

Portman’s character is very similar to the role she played in *[Black Swan](https://vinko.squarespace.com/blog/2011/02/27/movie-review-black-swan-2010)* that won her the Best Actress title in 2011. The actor who stood out in the movie was the young [Charlie Tahan](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm2253071/), brother of [Daisy Tahan](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm2394093/), who played a very believable boy stuck in the middle of a bad divorce and remarriage of his father, played by the TV actor, [Scott Cohen](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0169753/).

The plot of the movie is simple and Portman’s character is the “other woman” who caused the break up of her boss’ marriage. Ironically that was the same reason her mother divorced her father. Portman also struggle with the guilt of her 2 weeks old baby dying in her arms.

I was more mesmerized by Tahan’s character and his struggle to cope with the changes in his life more than Portman’s character.

For Portman’s fan this may be a must see but for me I think Tahan is an up and coming actor we can expect much from. Hopefully he does not fall into the traditional child actor predicament like [Gary Coleman](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0171041/) or [Dana Plato](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0686818/), but shines like [Drew Barrymore](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000106/).

This is a good movie to go to if you don’t have anything else to choose from at the box office.
