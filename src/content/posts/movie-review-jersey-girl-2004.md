Today I saw the movie [Jersey Girl](http://www.apple.com/trailers/miramax/jersey_girl/) at the Olympian Broadway theatre, it was a great movie with very good cast and tones of cameos. Another one from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.php) list.

Raquel Castro played Ben Affleck’s character’s (Ollie) daughter. This movie is her first motion picture, but I can see that there will be many more to come.

The plot is simple but really makes one think about that are really important in their own lives.
