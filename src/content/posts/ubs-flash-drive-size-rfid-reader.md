A mainland Chinese manufacture of [RFID](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rfid "Definition of RFID") products, [Daily RFID Co. Ltd.](http://www.rfid-in-china.com "Daily RFID Co. Ltd. web site"), released an ultra small RFID reader. Its form factor is perfect for the ad hoc RFID scanning purpose. It has a range of 30mm to 70mm and it compatible with13.56MHz frequency chips.

Wouldn’t it be great for a teacher to plug into his notebook this RFID reader, and the students in the classroom will automatically register in his attendance application.
