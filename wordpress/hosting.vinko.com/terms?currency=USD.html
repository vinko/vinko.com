Registration and renewal of .EU domain names

EURid (The European Registry of Internet Domain names) requires the registrant contact of .EU domain names to be situated in the European Union. If this requirement is not met, the domain registration may be rejected or revoked. Due to limitations imposed by EURid, the manual renewal of .EU domain names is not possible. Renewing .EU domain names is done by setting an "automated renewal" flag. The Provider will enable this setting by default. If a client does not want to renew a .EU domain name, they need to contact the Provider at least 30 days before the domain's expiration date so that this flag can be disabled.

All .EU domain name registrations need to match the EURid requirements listed at http://www.eurid.eu/.

WHOIS privacy terms

The domain WHOIS privacy feature replaces the data in the WHOIS output of your domain with details of SureSupport.com. You are still the owner of the domain name, and you can turn off the WHOIS privacy feature at any time.

  • Delivery of emails: With WHOIS privacy feature enabled, all email addresses (Registrant, Administrative, Technical, Billing) will be replaced with a randomly generated email address @privacy.suresupport.com. This email address will forward all messages sent to the WHOIS contacts to your official registrant email address.
  • Delivery of regular mail: We do not guarantee the proper delivery of regular mail and packages sent to the WHOIS privacy address. We may review the deliveries and contact you about them only if we find it necessary.
  • Escrow of the details of the official domain owner: When the WHOIS privacy feature is enabled, your own contact information will NOT be escrowed.
  • Editing domain contacts: If you use WHOIS Protection, you must edit the domain data only through the Provider's interfaces. Editing domain contacts through another interface (for example, at the domain registrar) may lead to unpredicted issues with the contact information.
  • WHOIS verification with Private WHOIS: Each enabling or disabling of the Private WHOIS service, as well as each generation of new privacy email, may require a verification of the WHOIS data, as per ICANN regulations. A verification message will be sent to your real registrant email address, and you need to follow the verification link in it within 15 days, to avoid disruption of the domain service.
