This afternoon I saw the movie “[The Pursuit of Happiness](http://www.apple.com/trailers/sony_pictures/thepursuitofhappyness/)” at UA Time Square, Causeway Bay.

This is the first serious movie that I see [Will Smith](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000226/) in. I had always enjoyed Will Smith movies like:

- [Bad Boys](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0112442/) (1995)
- [Independence Day](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0116629/) (1996)
- [Men in Black](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0119654/) (1997)
- [Enemy of the State](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0120660/) (1998)
- [Men in Black II](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0120912/) (2002)
- [Bad Boys II](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0172156/) (2003)
- [I, Robot](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0343818/) (2004)

There are a few bombs like:

- [Wild Wild West](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0120891/) (1999)
- [Hitch](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0386588/) (2005)

I am very impress with Smith’s performance. He had a very believable performance of the life of Chris Gardner. Again proofing Smith is a Hollywood super star with a diverse acting talents.
