This evening I saw the movie “Red Eye” on DVD.

This movie reminds me of [Harrison Ford’s](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000148/) Jack Ryan series, but not as professionally made. It does have the same elements of conspiracy, but the character developments were weak and felt rushed.

I really should stick to [My Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.php "Must See Movies list") list, when choosing movies to watch.
