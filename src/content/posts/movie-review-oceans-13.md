This evening I saw the movie “[Ocean’s 13](http://www.apple.com/trailers/wb/oceans13/)” at UA Time Square, Causeway Bay.

Unfortunately, this is yet another movie that fell into the sequel hole. In my opinion the only one from the series that’s original was the first one “[Ocean’s 11](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0240772/)“, which was a remake of the [1960 original](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0054135/).

Yes, the clothes on [George Clooney](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000123/) and [Brad Pitt](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000093/), but other than that there is really no surprise in this one and the plot is even weaker.
