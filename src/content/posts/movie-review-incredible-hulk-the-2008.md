This evening I saw the movie “[The Incredible Hulk](http://www.apple.com/trailers/universal/theincrediblehulk/ "The Incredible Hulk trailer")” at UA Cinema, Time Square, Hong Kong.

Another sequel to the [Incredible Hulk TV series](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0077031/ "Incredible Hulk TV Series details") remake, the original remake was [*Hulk*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0286716/ "Hulk details") in 2003. This time they change the actor playing [Bruce Banner](http://www.imdb.com/character/ch0007099/) ([Edward Norton](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001570/)).

Not sure if it was due to my expectations; or lack of, going into the movie, this was a pretty good movie. As many of you know, the story of *The Incredible Hulk* has been told many times over and over. The hero, Banner fights all odds to overcome the misguided impressions that people have of him when he is the Hulk. When all he wanted to do is to return normal and  live a regular life.

As always with any Marvel comic there is a villain, this time played by Tim Roth. Where he started as the hated villain in [*The Musketeer*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0246544/), [Invincible](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0245171/), [Pulp Fiction](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0110912/) and many more.

In the end, they did something no other movies had done, that is to tie two different Marvel movies together to give the audience a hint of what to come.

It is a definite entertaining one to watch. Take the kids, but watch out, you may have to deal with their nightmares afterwards.
