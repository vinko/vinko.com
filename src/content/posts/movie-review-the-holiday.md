This evening I saw the movie “[The Holiday](http://www.apple.com/trailers/sony_pictures/theholiday/)” at UA Cinema, Whampoa.

The cast chosen for this movie were just perfect, [Cameron Diaz](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000139/), [Kate Winslet](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000701/), [Jude Law](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000179/), [Jack Black](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0085312/) and [Eli Wallach](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0908919/). The characters fit the stereotypes of what majority of people have for these actors. Some may say it was to extreme, but to say anymore will give away the movie.

All I can say is, if you’re a women in a relationship, a women in a bad relationship, a women in the end of a relationship… oh well… if you are a women you should go see this movie. If you are a sensitive man; even if it is self proclaimed, you should go see this movie also.

If you have seen my previous review, then you will too be surprised that I give this movie a higher penguin rating than “[Casino Royale](http://www.vinko.com/weblog/2006/12/movie-review-casino-royale.shtml)“. It is mainly due to this movie’s entertainment factor and Casino Royale was very disappointing to me.
