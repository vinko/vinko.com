This is a quick way to access the International Red Cross donation site in support of the victims of the March 11th Japan earthquake, tsunami and nuclear disaster.

Simply use your QR Code reader app (like “[QuickMark QR Code Reader](http://blog.vinko.com/link/quickmark/)” on iOS) on your smartphone to decode the [link](http://www.jrc.or.jp/english/relief/l4/Vcms4_00002070.html).
