This evening I went to see the movie “[She’s The Man](http://www.apple.com/trailers/dreamworks/shestheman/)” at JP Cinema, Causeway Bay.

I guess this was the first time I had ever been to a movie and felt that it was a “girl’s movie”. The only other movie where I felt this way was “[Legally Blonde](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0250494/)“, but I did not pay to see that.

I can imagine “girls” would react in such a way, as portrayed, given the situations presented in the movie, and because of this it earn an extra penguin in my rating for this movie.

So if you are a female and like this genre of movies, you will also enjoy this one. For the rest, you know my rating system… remember to subtract the penguin earned.
