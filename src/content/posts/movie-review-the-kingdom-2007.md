This evening I saw the movie “[The Kingdom](http://www.apple.com/trailers/universal/thekingdom/)” at JP Theater, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong.

[Jamie Foxx](http://imdb.com/name/nm0004937/) was his usual self, playing the good guy he had in the past, with just a mediocre performance. The other members of the cast, [Jennifer Garner](http://imdb.com/name/nm0004950/) and [Jason Bateman](http://imdb.com/name/nm0000867/) did not help either.

The story of the movie is quite straight forward with a simple lesson in the end. Although, this movie is on my “Must See” list, after seeing it myself, it can now be moved to my “Can Miss” list.
