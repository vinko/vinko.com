This evening I saw the movie “[Miss Potter](http://www.apple.com/trailers/weinstein/misspotter/)” at UA Cinema, Time Square, Causeway Bay.

This is a movie about the English writer, [Beatrix Potter](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beatrix_Potter) who created the popular and famous “Peter the Rabit” story book character and many others. The Oscar winning, [Renee Zellweger](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000250/) played Beatrix Potter, and the Golden Globe nominee [Ewan McGregor](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000191/) played Potter’s junior publisher, Norman Warne. They were quite good.

Each and every time I see a Zellweger movie I am surprised and impressed with the wide range of characters she is able to portray and made believable on the big screen. My first was

- “[Bridget Jones’s Diary](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0243155/)” in 2001
- “[Chicago](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0299658/)” in 2002
- “[Down with Love](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0309530/)” in 2003
- “[Bridget Jones: The Edge of Reason](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0317198/)” in 2004

Like her other movies, and this one in particular is very suitable for the whole family.
