Tonight I watched *[Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind](http://click.linksynergy.com/fs-bin/stat?id=AYDmH5A9AOI&offerid=146261&type=3&subid=0&tmpid=1826&RD_PARM1=http%253A%252F%252Fitunes.apple.com%252FWebObjects%252FMZStore.woa%252Fwa%252FviewMovie%253Fid%253D303440550%2526s%253D143441%2526uo%253D6%2526partnerId%253D30)* staring Jim Carrey at Silvercord in Tsim Sa Tsui. Not one of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.php) list.

What can I say? Unfortunately it is yet another one of those movies, where a famous, successful and well liked movie star try something different. My last experience of that was with Bill Murray in “[Lost in Translation](http://click.linksynergy.com/fs-bin/stat?id=AYDmH5A9AOI&offerid=146261&type=3&subid=0&tmpid=1826&RD_PARM1=http%253A%252F%252Fitunes.apple.com%252FWebObjects%252FMZStore.woa%252Fwa%252FviewMovie%253Fid%253D303440550%2526s%253D143441%2526uo%253D6%2526partnerId%253D30)“.

It is definitely not a typical Jim Carrey movie that he had made in the past.

Although, I had place this movie in my “Must See Movies”, I will now remove it after watching it myself. To say the least I did not enjoy it, may be it was too deep and intellectual for me, you may decide that yourself.
