This evening I saw the movie *[Duplicity](http://www.apple.com/trailers/universal/duplicity/ "Duplicity trailer")* at UA Cinema, Time Square, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. Another one of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.html) list.

I always enjoy movies with [Julia Roberts](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000210/) and this one was no exception, but she was the only one any good. I never liked [Clive Owen](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0654110/) and his movies and I think this would have been a better movie if the cast were different. [George Clooney](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000123/) and [Brad Pitt](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000093/) would have been much better choices as Roberts’ partner in the movie.

It is a movie about corporate espionage between two rival corporation. Where Roberts and Owen are playing both sides for their self gain.

When the movie begins; actually during the first 15 minutes of the movie, you may be lost looking for the plot to begin. This is not surprising as you are dropped in the middle of a story about two spies. It is not until two thirds into the movie that you get the whole plot and back story. I guess this was the writers’ way of keeping suspense for a very weak plot.

If it was not for Roberts I would have given this 1 penguin like I did with most other Owen’s movies.
