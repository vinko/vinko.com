This evening I saw the movie “[Hellboy 2](http://www.apple.com/trailers/universal/hellboy2thegoldenarmy/ "Hellboy 2 trailer")” at AMC Theater, Pacific Place, Admiralty, Hong Kong.

I did not go watch [Hellboy](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0167190/), the first episode in this franchise, at the cinema, but caught it on HBO. I did not find the first movie that interesting, so I did not put this sequel in my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.php) list.

Recently many of my friends gave it rave reviews after watching the latest episode in the franchise; as did [RT](http://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/hellboy_2_the_golden_army/), so it made me question my decision for not wanting to see this movie.

Tonight I was presently surprised by the movie. As with all sequels, it is always hard to balance the need to provide just enough background store for the audiences and keep the fans interested. Especially when the previous episode was four years ago. I think the director (screenplay and writer), [Guillermo del Toro](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0868219/) achieved that very well.

Another thing they did correctly, was to keep the main casts from the first episode, giving the movie a continuum that is much needed for sequels. [Ron Perlman](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000579/) again played the adult Hellboy, [Selma Blair](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0004757/) played Liz Sherman, both [Doug Jones](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0427964/) and [Brian Steele](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0824498/) were chameleons of characters.

The special effect was done very well, it shows what four years of technology advancements in CG can do for a movie. The CG seamlessly integrated with the live action of the movie. There were many background characters and details in the sets and costumes, giving the audience much eye candy other than the story. There were one scene which reminded me of the bar in [Star Wars](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0076759/) (Star Wars: Episode IV – A New Hope).

The story had the feel of [Fantastic Four](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0120667/) the magic of [Harry Potter](http://www.imdb.com/character/ch0000985/) the mystery of [Indiana Jones](http://www.imdb.com/character/ch0001063/) and [National Treasure](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0368891/). Overall a very enjoyable movie to bring the kids to.
