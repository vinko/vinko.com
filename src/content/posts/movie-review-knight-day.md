This afternoon I saw the movie *[Knight and Day](http://www.apple.com/trailers/fox/knightandday/ "Knight and Day trailer")* at AMC Cinemas, Pacific Place, Admiralty, Hong Kong. One of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/mustsee) list.

The leads in this movie are [Tom Cruise](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000129/ "Tom Cruise Profile") and [Cameron Diaz](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000139/ "Cameron Diaz Profile") who had one of their best performances of both careers.

Cruise had matured as an actor and this time he did not play an amateur spy role as he did in the Mission Impossible franchises. Although Diaz still plays her cute girl next door character as she did in the Charlie Angels franchises, this time she has a much more mature role. This may be due to both actors being older than when both stared in the respective franchises.

The stunts were impressive, fresh and believable. The plot is not that original and is like most spy movies, with reasonable amount of twists and suspense to keep the audience engaged. Additional entertainment factor was the results of the chemistry that Cruise and Diaz portrayed which also served to give the movie the comedy aspect that made this movie fun.

If you are looking for a light romance suspenseful comedy, then this is the movie for you.
