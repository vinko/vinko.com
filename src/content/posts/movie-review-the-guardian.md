This evening I saw the movie “[The Guardian](http://www.apple.com/trailers/touchstone/theguardian/index.html)” at UA Cinema, Time Square, Causeway Bay.

It has been a while since I’ve seen any [Kevin Costner](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000126/) movies. I was going to watch “[The Upside of Anger](http://www.apple.com/trailers/newline/the_upside_of_anger/)” (at [IMDB](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0365885/)) and “[Rumor Has It…](http://www.apple.com/trailers/wb/rumor_has_it/)” (at [IMDB](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0398375/)) last years (2005), but never got around to them.

The last movie I saw Costner in was “[The Postman](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0119925/)” and he was not that good. So I was presently surprise to see Costner’s performance in this movie, but then it may be because I did not have too high of expectation.

The other characters in the movie except Ben Randall’s (Costner’s character) wife (“[Sela Ward](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000688/)“) were actors whom we were accustom to staring in comedy movies, so it was a bit hard, for me, to see them in serious roles. Through out the movie I was expecting these other actors to break out in some sort of comedy skid.

Overall, this movie was enjoyable and had good mixture or humor, romance and drama. I was taken by surprise to see Costner carried the movie mostly on his own.

So, if you have time give this one a try, but if there is a choice you can either save this one for next time round or wait for the DVD.
