This evening I saw the movie *[Prometheus](http://trailers.apple.com/trailers/fox/prometheus/)* at AMC Theater, Pacific Place, Admiralty, Hong Kong. One of the movies from my Must See Movies list, and the following is my movie review.

This [Ridley Scott](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000631/) movie is a prequel to the [Alien](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0078748/) franchise. Although the first Alien is the only movie in the series that Scott directed. For fans of the Alien franchise they may find this movie has many unanswered questions.

After the first quarter of the movie the audience will recognize the aliens from the previous Alien franchise. Similar to the other movies in the franchise there is also a heroine, in this movie it is Elizabeth Shaw, played by [Noomi Rapace](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0636426). Rapace may be familiar to many of you as she gain critical acclaim with her role as Lisbeth Salander in [The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt1132620/) and its subsequent sequels.
