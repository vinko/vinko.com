This evening I saw the movie “[War (2007) (aka “Rogue Assassin”)](http://www.apple.com/trailers/lions_gate/war/)” at Cinepex, Market Square, Markham, Ontario, Canada

Having been away from North American cinemas for some time. I wanted to watch a movie while I was in Toronto. Unfortunately, due to the amount of time I had, I was not able to find a movie that I want to watch at one of the better cinemas.

I had always like [Jet Li](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001472/) movies, when combined with [Jason Statham](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0005458/) and [John Lone](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0518821/) I was expecting a great movie with many amazing fight scenes. This was where a mistake, since it placed my expectation for the movie quite high. In foresight it was too high.

I think this is the first movie in which Statham had a lot of dialogue. For some reason I find that every time Statham spoke in the movie I had a real difficulty hearing what he was saying.

Fortunately the fight scenes were great as typical Jet Li movies, this may be because its martial art directions were by Corey Yuen, the fight choreographer of most of Jet Li’s previous movies.

The car driven by Li’s character was very nice. It is a [Spyker C8 Spyder](http://www.spykercars.nl/?pag=8). It it was not for the twisted plot and the cars in the movie I would have given it a even lower Penguin rating.
