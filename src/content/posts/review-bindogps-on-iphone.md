This is another Hong Kong built iPhone application from the growing iPhone development community here in Hong Kong. It is the newest among these applications: [Darkroom](http://click.linksynergy.com/fs-bin/stat?id=AYDmH5A9AOI&offerid=146261&type=3&subid=0&tmpid=1826&RD_PARM1=http%253A%252F%252Fitunes.apple.com%252FWebObjects%252FMZStore.woa%252Fwa%252FviewSoftware%253Fid%253D300282121%2526mt%253D8%2526uo%253D6%2526partnerId%253D30 "Darkroom Premium on iTunes") (currently not compatible with iPhone firmware 3.0), [Finger](http://blog.vinko.com/2009/03/17/review-finger-on-the-iphone/ "Finger Review") and [HK Movie](http://click.linksynergy.com/fs-bin/stat?id=AYDmH5A9AOI&offerid=146261&type=3&subid=0&tmpid=1826&RD_PARM1=http%253A%252F%252Fitunes.apple.com%252FWebObjects%252FMZStore.woa%252Fwa%252FviewSoftware%253Fid%253D303206353%2526mt%253D8%2526uo%253D6%2526partnerId%253D30 "Hong Kong Movie on iTunes"). The latter I will have an upcoming review.

[![Bindo Marker Photo](/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/marker-photo.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/marker-photo.png)BindoGPS is great when you want to record the track you took during your hike, or if you’re a runner you can use it to supplement your training; unfortunately, it does not yet allow the export of tracks in GPX or KML format. Although, [BindoGPS.com](http://bindogps.com "BindoGPS web site") has just added the ability to see the tracks you’ve recorded using the BindoGPS iPhone application on your iPhone.

BindoGPS’ ability to use either a photo from the iPhone’s photo albums or instantly capture it with the iPhone built in camera, as a waypoint is particularly useful if you want to record visual memory of your hike or conditions of a location during your run.

[![Bindo Track Details](/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/track-details.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/track-details.png)Additional benefits to hikers is its track details view. Where it shows the user’s current speed, direction and altitude. This is accomplished using the geo-coordinates and the history of the track, so this feature works even on the previous generations of iPhones (ie. iPhone, iPhone 3G) and iPod Touch.

[![Bindo Waypoint List](/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/waypoint-list.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/waypoint-list.png)The list of tracks (trips) you made with BindoGPS can be seen including a summary of distance travelled and direction of the track.

This also make it easy to backtrack on a track to its starting point. Especially with the iPhone 3GS’s built in compass.

This useful little program is available on [Apple iTunes App Store](http://click.linksynergy.com/fs-bin/stat?id=AYDmH5A9AOI&offerid=146261&type=3&subid=0&tmpid=1826&RD_PARM1=http%253A%252F%252Fitunes.apple.com%252FWebObjects%252FMZStore.woa%252Fwa%252FviewSoftware%253Fid%253D321201843%2526mt%253D8%2526uo%253D6%2526partnerId%253D30 "BindoGPS on iTunes") for USD4.99.
