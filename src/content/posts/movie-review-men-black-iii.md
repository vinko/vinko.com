This evening I saw the movie *[Men In Black III](http://trailers.apple.com/trailers/sony_pictures/meninblack3/ "Men In Black III trailer")* at AMC Theater, Pacific Place, Admiralty, Hong Kong. One of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/mustsee "Must See Movies list") list, and the following is my movie review.

It has been 10 years since the Men In Black franchise has a new episode. Not sure if it is worth the wait. Like most sequels, subsequence episodes do not have the original’s surprise and freshness, as a result the audiences are normally very critical. Unfortunately this episode did not live up to expectations.

[Will Smith](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000226/) again plays “agent J” whose partner, “agent K” (staring “Tommy Lee Jones” and “Josh Brolin” plays the young version) is in need of his help, but to do so Smith has to go back in time.

There are some surprises but at the end of the movie it felt the writers are running out of originality and did not know how to end the story.

If you have other choices at the box office you will want to save Men In Black for iTunes rental.
