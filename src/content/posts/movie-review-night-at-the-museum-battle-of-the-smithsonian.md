This afternoon I saw the movie *[Night At the Museum: Battle of the Smithsonian](http://www.apple.com/trailers/fox/nightatthemuseumbattleofthesmithsonian/ "Angels & Demons trailer")* at AMC Theater, Pacific Place, Admiralty, Hong Kong. Another one of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.html) list.

It was a typical performance by [Ben Stiller](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001774/) that was nothing special. On the other hand his supporting casts were quite impressive, namely [Hank Azaria](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000279/), [Amy Adams](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0010736/) and [Ricky Gervais](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0315041/). With Azaria carrying most of the movie by playing 3 characters.

I enjoyed Stiller in the [*Meet the Parents*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0212338/) franchise, first with [*Meet the Parents*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0212338/) then *[Meet the Fockers](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0290002/)* and expect to enjoy *[Little Fockers](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0970866/)* in 2011. The other movies Stiller in were not that great and I have concluded that [Robert De Niro](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000134/) was the one who carried the [*Meet the Parents*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0212338/) franchise to its success.

This latest movie further supports my hypothesis and I will now question any future decisions to add more Stiller movies to my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.html) list. Of course that will not stop me from adding these upcoming movies due to the other actors.

If you’re looking for a mindless entertainment and had no other choices at the theater they can be your choice, but like my ratings legend suggest, you should get this movie off iTunes.
