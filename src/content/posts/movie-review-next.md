This evening I saw the movie “[Next](http://www.apple.com/trailers/paramount/next/)” at UA Time Square, Causeway Bay.

This movie is amazing. It is the first movie I’ve seen that did not require an ending.

The writer and producer [Gary Goldman](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0325778/) co-authored the screenplay with [Jonathan Hensleigh](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0378144/) and [Paul Bernbaum](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0076602/), who based the story on [Philip K. Dick’s](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001140/) novel, “[The Golden Man](http://www.amazon.com/gp/redirect.html?ie=UTF8&location=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.amazon.com%2FGolden-Man-Philip-K-Dick%2Fdp%2F0417062001%3Fie%3DUTF8%26s%3Dbooks%26qid%3D1185297680%26sr%3D1-1&tag=vinkocom-20&linkCode=ur2&camp=1789&creative=9325)“.

The movie has enough suspense and action to keep you on the edge of your seat with twists and turns to keep you guessing until the “end”. Although, if it wasn’t yet another terrorist-against-the-United-States movie, I would have given it the 5 penguins rating that it deserves.

Without giving away the plot, I do have one advice before going into the movie; this movie can easily be classified as a “Sci-fi Thriller”. So don’t over analyze it and just sit back and enjoy.
