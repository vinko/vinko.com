This evening I saw the movie “[Night at the Museum](http://www.apple.com/trailers/fox/nightatthemuseum/)” at Ocean Terminal, Golden Harvest Theater, Tsim Sa Tsui.

Another typical [Ben Stiller](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001774/) style movie. Like the characters he played in the “[Meet the Parents](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0212338/)” series, his character in this movie is also common and a bit nerdy, who is down in his luck.

There were a lot of famous cast to support Stiller’s performance, like his opposite from Meet the Parents, “[Owen Wilson](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0005562/), [Robin Williams](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000245/), [Dick Van Dyke](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001813/) and [Mickey Rooney](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001682/).

Even with all the big names supporting casts the movie is only barely entertaining. Fortunately, I have the large quantities of [CGI](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer-generated_imagery "Computer-generated imagery") to fill the my remainder lack of interest for the movie.
