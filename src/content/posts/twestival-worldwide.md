[![Twestival HK 2010 Logo](/wp-content/uploads/2010/03/twestival-hk-logo.png)](http://hongkong.twestival.com "Twestival Hong Kong Event")Starting at 16:00 (GMT+0800, aka HK time) close to 200 cities around the world will began their local [Twestival](http://twestival.com/ "Twestival Events Worldwide").

This year the event is to raise money for [Concern Worldwide](http://twestival.com/concern/ "Concern Worldwide page on Twestival") in support of their efforts with educating children around the world.

If you cannot make it to the event in [Hong Kong](http://hongkong.twestival.com "Twestival HK site") at 19:00 (GMT+0800) [Staunton’s Wine Bar & Cafe](http://www.stauntonsgroup.com/staunton/ "Staunton's Wine Bar & Cafe site") or at your local city, please make your donations via [PayPal](http://paypal.com "PayPal web site") using the FLASH widget on the home page of the [Twestival Hong Kong](http://hongkong.twestival.com "Twestival Hong Kong Event") site.

You can also come back here and watch the LIVE [UStream broadcast](http://ustream.tv/channel/twestival-hk "UStream Live Broadcast of Twestival HK") of the event starting 21:30 (GMT+0800).
