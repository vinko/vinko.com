I just saw the following tweet in [my Twitter stream](http://twitter.com/vinko "Vinko's Twitter Stream").

![SCMP Tweet](/wp-content/uploads/2009/03/scmp-tweet.png)

For those of you who does not know [SCMP](http://www.scmp.com "SCMP web site") stands for “South China Morning Post” the only major English newspaper left in Hong Kong.

Does SCMP realize they are a news agency. If there is a story about police in China arresting people carrying GPS devices as spy, then we want to know about it now, today. We don’t need a preview of “story to come tomorrow” so you can hope to sell more newspaper tomorrow.
