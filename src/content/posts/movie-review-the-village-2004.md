Yesterday afternoon I went to see the movie “[The Village](http://www.apple.com/trailers/touchstone/the_village/)” at Broadway Cinema, Olympian Plaza.

I did not plan to go see this movie, and it did not make it onto my “[Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/mustsee.html)” list. To tell you the truth this was not the kind of movies I would have gone to see on my own accord, but I was presently surprise.

Actually, “surprise” is what this movie is all about. But then what would you expect for a movie coming from the director, M. Night Shyamalan, who also directed: “[The Sixth Sense](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0167404/)” and “[Unbreakable](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0217869/)“.

If I tell you any more I will ruin the whole plot for you. Like the other two movies by Shyamalan, you should avoid hearing any more about it before watching the movie yourself.
