[![AutoStich Logo](/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/picture-14.png)](http://click.linksynergy.com/fs-bin/stat?id=AYDmH5A9AOI&offerid=146261&type=3&subid=0&tmpid=1826&RD_PARM1=http%253A%252F%252Fitunes.apple.com%252FWebObjects%252FMZStore.woa%252Fwa%252FviewSoftware%253Fid%253D318944927%2526mt%253D8%2526uo%253D6%2526partnerId%253D30)I was introduced to this cool little iPhone application, [AutoStich](http://click.linksynergy.com/fs-bin/stat?id=AYDmH5A9AOI&offerid=146261&type=3&subid=0&tmpid=1826&RD_PARM1=http%253A%252F%252Fitunes.apple.com%252FWebObjects%252FMZStore.woa%252Fwa%252FviewSoftware%253Fid%253D318944927%2526mt%253D8%2526uo%253D6%2526partnerId%253D30), that enables easy creation of panorama (pano) photos on the go.

[![AutoStich Select Photos](/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/autostich-select.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2009/07/autostich-select.png)Most pano maker applications on the Mac or on the iPhone (like [PanoLab Pro](http://click.linksynergy.com/fs-bin/stat?id=AYDmH5A9AOI&offerid=146261&type=3&subid=0&tmpid=1826&RD_PARM1=http%253A%252F%252Fitunes.apple.com%252FWebObjects%252FMZStore.woa%252Fwa%252FviewSoftware%253Fid%253D294770811%2526mt%253D8%2526uo%253D6%2526partnerId%253D30)) require the user to painstakingly aligns the different photos that make up the final pano image, including skewing any photos to adjust for the distortion (parallax), a side effect of not rotating the iPhone on the pano pivot point of the lens.

Amazingly, AutoStich does not require any assistance from the user, plus it is able to reorder the set of photos to their correct order, to ensure a smooth alignment of the final pano image.

At the price of FREE this little iPhone application is definitely worth more than its developer is asking for.
