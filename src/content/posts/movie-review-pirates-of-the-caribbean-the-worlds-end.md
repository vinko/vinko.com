This evening I saw the movie “[Pirates of the Caribbean: The World’s End](http://www.apple.com/trailers/disney/piratesofthecaribbeanatworldsend/)” at UA Cinema, Time Square, Causeway Bay.

The last of the Pirates of the Caribbean series. It is definitely a good one, much better than the second one, “[Pirates of the Caribbean: Dead Man’s Chest](http://imdb.com/title/tt0383574/)“. Before I went to see this latest episode I decided to rewatch “Dead Man’s Chest”, but I fell asleep while doing so. I do admit that I did not have too high of an expectation for “The World’s End” before I went to see it.

Again “[Johnny Depp](http://imdb.com/name/nm0000136/)” was at his usual best. The character he seems to unable to come out of since “[The Curse of the Black Pearl](http://imdb.com/title/tt0325980/)“.

This episode has the usual [CGI](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer-generated_imagery) that we come to expect in Pirates of the Caribbean.

All in all this was an entertaining movie and deserves the 4 penguins that I had given it.
