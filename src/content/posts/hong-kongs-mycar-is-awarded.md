The [Hong Kong made MyCar](http://blog.vinko.com/2005/10/04/first-car-to-be-made-in-hong-kong/ "Hong Kong made MyCar") that I told you almost 3 years ago has won an award from the GreenFleet magazine, who has given it the “[electric vehicle of the year award](http://www.greenfleet.net/content/view/400/113/ "GreenFleet Magazine Award")“. Well done!

I wish it is available in Hong Kong, we sure need something like this to improve the pollution level.
