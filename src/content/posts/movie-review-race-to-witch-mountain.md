This afternoon I saw the movie *[Race to Witch Mountain](http://www.apple.com/trailers/disney/racetowitchmountain/ "Race to Witch Mountain trailer")* at UA Cinema, Time Square, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong. Another one of the movies from my [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.php) list.

I went into the movie without much expectation, since it was staring [Dwayne Johnson](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0425005/ "Dwayne Johnson profile"), famous for his WWE character “The Rock” and his motion picture debut in [The Mummy Returns](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0209163/ "The Mummy Returns details"), as “[Mathayus the Scorpion King](http://www.imdb.com/character/ch0002680/ "Scorpion King details")“.

The movie had two child actors, [AnnaSophia Robb](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm1455681/ "AnnaSophia Robb  profile") and [Alexander Ludwig](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm1573253/ "Alexander Ludwig profile"), and they were just as bad as Johnson. The only excuse Robb and Ludwig has is that they were playing aliens who are new to human speak, in this case English, but Johnson does not have this excuse, as he was playing an English speaking American.

I guess the blame cannot complete go to Johnson, Robb and Ludwig. The play writes, [Matt Lopez](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm2188941/ "Matt Lopez profile") and [Mark Bomback](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0093560/ "Mark Bomback profile") should take some of the blame these bad dialogs. This would not have been a total surprise if I had known Bomback was the one who wrote the screenplay for [*Live Free or Die Hard*](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0337978/). Next time I will look at the “play writes” in additional to leading actors and director in a movie before deciding on whether to include it in my” [Must See Movies](http://www.vinko.com/php/phpmov/index.php) list.
