[![Twestival HK 2010 Logo](/wp-content/uploads/2010/03/twestival-hk-logo.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2010/03/twestival-hk-logo.png)On March 25 a group of Twitter users will join over 200 cities/countries around the world for Twestival. An excuse to get together and network in person with all the people you have exchanged tweets with over the years. While at the same time have chance to win great prices and raise money for [Concern Worldwide](http://www.concern.net/ "Concern Worldwide web site").

Thanks to Staunton’s Group, this year’s [Hong Kong](http://riotvine.com/riotvine-member/show/twestival-hk-de649276/ "Twestival HK Info") event will be held at [Staunton’s Wine Bar & Cafe](http://www.stauntonsgroup.com/ "Staunton's Wine Bar & Cafe web site").

By the way there will be small quantities of the limited edition [G.O.D.](http://www.god.com.hk/ "GOD web site") designed and sponsored t-shirts available at the door. So remember to get there early to ensure you get one.

Tickets are available [online](http://www.amiando.com/Twestival2010_HongKong "Twestival HK 2010 Online Ticketing") or at the door of Staunton’s Wine Bar & Cafe on the night.

See you all there.
